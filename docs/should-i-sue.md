# Should I Sue? — copy for ShouldISue.org

Ready-to-paste text for the website. Should I Sue? is part of the Legal AI Skills plugin and runs in **Claude Code** (the CLI, or the Code tab in the Claude desktop app) and **Codex**.

## Install prompt

```text
Install Legal AI Skills from https://github.com/rohasnagpal/legal-ai-skills and say "Should I Sue".
```

## How it works

1. **Install the plugin.** Open Claude Code or Codex and paste the prompt above. Node.js 18 or later is needed for the built-in research and calculation tools.
2. **Add your evidence.** Put your contracts, emails, messages, screenshots, financial records and legal notices in one folder.
3. **Ask one question.** Open a new session in that folder and type: *Should I sue?*
4. **Let the AI legal team investigate.** Specialised, jurisdiction-aware AI agents examine the legal claims, the strengths and weaknesses of your case, your evidence, legal risks, costs and consequences, your chances of success, and practical alternatives.
5. **Get a reasoned recommendation.** You get litigation, arbitration, mediation, negotiation, a regulatory or consumer complaint, or walking away, with reasons, key risks, any urgent deadline, and next steps.

The final decision is yours.

## Privacy

The AI reads only the files in the folder you open. Your documents stay on your computer and in your AI provider's environment. The built-in tools run on your machine, and research connectors receive only search terms. Check your AI provider's data terms before adding confidential documents.

## Not legal advice

Should I Sue? gives an informed assessment, not legal advice. It covers India, the US and the UK in depth; other countries get general guidance only. Talk to a qualified lawyer before filing, signing or sending anything, especially if a deadline is close.
