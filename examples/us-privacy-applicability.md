# Demo: US privacy applicability

## Example instruction

> A fictional online service has customers in several US States, processes account, location and support data, and sells only to businesses. Identify the privacy-law questions before we build the compliance plan.

## Illustrative consolidated output

### Scope statement

“US privacy law” is not one regime. Coverage depends on the States involved, statutory thresholds, the entity and data exemptions, sector, processing purpose and whether individuals act in a consumer, employee or business-contact capacity.

### Applicability matrix

| Question | Evidence required | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Relevant States | Customer and individual locations | Determines which State regimes require testing |
| Thresholds | Revenue and processing volumes by period | May determine entity-level coverage |
| Data role | Collection, sharing, sale and processor contracts | Affects duties, notices and contractual controls |
| Exemptions | Sector, entity and data-category facts | Exemptions differ and must not be assumed |

### Next actions

Build a State-by-State data and threshold table, verify each potentially applicable statute from official sources, then map confirmed duties to notices, rights operations, contracts, security and evidence.

### Verification status

No State law is labelled applicable on these incomplete fictional facts. Current statutes, amendments, regulations and enforcement guidance require live verification.
