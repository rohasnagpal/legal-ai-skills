# Demo: Indian divorce (mutual consent)

## Example instruction

> Hello Rohas. I married in Delhi in 2018 under Hindu rites. My husband and I both want a divorce and have lived apart since January. We have a five-year-old son. I want maintenance, custody of my son and my jewellery back. I haven't collected our income documents yet.

## How the firm handles it

1. **Managing Partner** sizes the matter as complex (divorce, maintenance, custody and property) and asks India Counsel for an intake assessment.
2. **India Counsel** confirms that Indian law applies, flags that the governing personal law must be confirmed from the form of marriage and the parties' religion, and identifies the family court with territorial jurisdiction.
3. **Family Lawyer** is appointed Matter Owner and chooses the mutual-consent branch of the [divorce workflow](../plugins/legal-ai-skills/workflows/divorce-and-matrimonial-proceedings.md), with the children stage.
4. The Family Lawyer sends one delegation request to the Real Estate Lawyer about transferring the jointly held flat. The Managing Partner dispatches it.

## Illustrative consolidated output

### Route

Mutual-consent divorce appears available if both spouses sign and the statutory separation and waiting periods are met. Those periods, and whether the waiting period can be waived, are marked for verification against the current law and recent authority.

### Safety

No violence is alleged. If that changes, protection takes priority over every other step.

### Maintenance and custody

Maintenance is shown as ranges because income documents are missing. The custody proposal centres on the child's welfare: the child lives with the mother, with a weekday and weekend schedule for the father and shared festivals.

### Property

An asset schedule separates the jewellery, which is claimed as stridhan with recovery evidence listed, from the jointly held flat, which is referred to the Real Estate Lawyer.

### Documents

A settlement outline, the joint petition checklist and a filing timeline.

### Verification status

Statutory periods, the court's filing requirements and any authority cited are marked unverified until checked against current primary sources. Signing the settlement and filing require a qualified advocate.
