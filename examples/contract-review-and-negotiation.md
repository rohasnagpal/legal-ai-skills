# Demo: contract review and negotiation

## Example instruction

> We represent the customer. Review this fictional three-year SaaS agreement. The supplier may suspend immediately, disclaims all data-loss liability and can increase fees annually without a cap. Prepare priority redlines and negotiation positions.

## Illustrative consolidated output

### Executive position

The draft transfers service-continuity, data-loss and pricing risk disproportionately to the customer. The first negotiation package should connect suspension, service levels, data protection, liability and exit assistance rather than negotiate each clause in isolation.

### Priority issues

| Priority | Issue | Customer impact | Proposed position |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| High | Immediate suspension | Business interruption without cure | Limit to material security or payment risk; require notice, cure and proportionate suspension |
| High | Data-loss exclusion | No meaningful remedy for supplier-controlled loss | Carve out confidentiality, data protection, gross negligence and restoration costs from the exclusion |
| Medium | Uncapped fee increases | Unbudgeted long-term cost | Fixed pricing for the initial term; objective capped index thereafter |

### Negotiation ladder

- Opening: notice and cure for all suspension; targeted liability carve-outs; no increase during the initial term.
- Fallback: emergency suspension only for documented imminent harm, with prompt restoration duties.
- Client decision required: acceptable aggregate liability cap and maximum renewal increase.

### Verification status

The commercial analysis is illustrative. Governing law, the complete agreement, schedules, security terms and the customer's approved risk limits would be required for final redlines.
