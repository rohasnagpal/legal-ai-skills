# Demo: M&A legal due diligence

## Example instruction

> We represent the buyer of a fictional software company. Review the supplied corporate records, top customer agreements, employment documents and IP schedule. Flag issues that could affect signing, price or closing.

## Illustrative consolidated output

### Executive summary

Three matters require transaction action: an incomplete founder IP assignment, change-of-control consent in a material customer contract and an option ledger that does not reconcile to the board approvals. None should be treated as resolved merely because the data room contains no contrary document.

### Issue register

| ID | Severity | Evidence | Transaction response |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| IP-01 | High | Founder assignment excludes pre-incorporation code | Obtain confirmatory assignment before signing or closing |
| CON-02 | High | Customer agreement requires prior written consent | Make consent a condition or allocate termination/revenue risk expressly |
| CORP-03 | Information gap | Option ledger differs from approval schedule | Reconcile grants, lapses and exercise status before fixing fully diluted ownership |

### Immediate requests

1. Complete repository contribution and assignment history.
2. Executed consent or agreed consent plan for the customer contract.
3. Board and shareholder approvals supporting every outstanding option.

### Verification status

This fictional output demonstrates evidence-linked findings. Registry status, governing law, litigation, tax and regulatory matters would remain outstanding unless separately verified.
