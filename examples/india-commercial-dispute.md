# Demo: India commercial dispute

## Example instruction

> Our fictional Indian supplier has unpaid invoices under a contract with a Delhi forum clause. Prepare the dispute route. The agreement, invoices and demand correspondence are supplied, but service dates and the debtor's assets are incomplete.

## Illustrative consolidated output

### Jurisdiction and scope

Indian law and a Delhi forum appear relevant from the fictional contract, subject to verification of execution, jurisdiction and the complete dispute-resolution clause. Delhi-specific court rules, pecuniary allocation, court fees and current filing requirements must be checked before filing.

### Recommended sequence

1. Reconcile the contract, delivery evidence, invoice ledger, credits and admissions.
2. Confirm limitation trigger dates and preserve alternative calculations.
3. Test whether mandatory pre-institution mediation applies and whether urgent interim relief is genuinely required.
4. Send a fact-specific demand without making unsupported fraud allegations.
5. Investigate recoverable assets before selecting litigation economics and settlement parameters.

### Evidence gaps

- proof and date of service of the existing demand;
- signed delivery records for two invoices;
- debtor asset and charge information; and
- current Delhi filing, fee and mediation requirements.

### Verification status

No final limitation date or filing-ready pleading can be given until trigger dates and current primary procedural sources are verified.
