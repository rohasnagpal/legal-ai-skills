# Demo: UK employment issue

## Example instruction

> A fictional employer wants to dismiss a remote worker described only as “UK based” after a performance process. Assess what must be established before advice is given.

## Illustrative consolidated output

### Jurisdiction gate

The worker's actual work location and employment connection must be established. England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland cannot be treated as procedurally identical, and contractual governing-law language is not necessarily conclusive of statutory protection.

### Required facts

- work location and pattern throughout employment;
- employing entity, contract, status and continuity dates;
- employer size and applicable policies;
- performance allegations, objectives, support, warnings and appeal rights;
- protected characteristics, leave, whistleblowing, health and safety or other protected activity; and
- proposed reason, decision-maker, date and forum risk.

### Provisional action plan

Preserve the performance record, pause any final recommendation until jurisdiction and protected-activity checks are complete, compare the process with contract and policy, and verify current statutory and tribunal requirements from official sources.

### Verification status

The example does not conclude that dismissal is fair or lawful. That requires complete facts, the correct UK jurisdiction and current-law verification.
