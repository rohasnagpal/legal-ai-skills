# DATA ANALYTICS SERVICES AGREEMENT (extract)

**Dated 3 February 2025**

**Supplier:** Brightline Analytics Ltd (company no. 11111111, fictional), London
**Customer:** Harbourside Retail Group Ltd (company no. 22222222, fictional), London

**Clause 4 – Services.** The Supplier shall deliver the customer-insights dashboard (Phase 1) by 30 April 2025 and monthly analytics reports (Phase 2) from May 2025.

**Clause 6 – Fees and payment.** The Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within 30 days of receipt. If the Customer disputes an invoice in good faith, it shall notify the Supplier in writing within 10 days of receipt, giving reasons, and pay the undisputed part.

**Clause 6.4 – Late payment.** Late payments carry interest at 4% per year above the Bank of England base rate.

**Clause 11 – Liability.** Each party's total liability is capped at the fees paid or payable in the 12 months before the claim.

**Clause 15 – Termination.** Either party may terminate on 60 days' written notice.

**Clause 19 – Disputes.**
19.1 The parties shall first try to resolve any dispute by negotiation between senior executives within 21 days of a written request.
19.2 Any dispute not so resolved shall be referred to and finally resolved by arbitration under the LCIA Rules. The seat shall be London. The tribunal shall consist of one arbitrator. The language shall be English.

**Clause 21 – Governing law.** This agreement is governed by the law of England and Wales.
