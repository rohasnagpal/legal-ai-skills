# AGREEMENT FOR SALE (extract)

**Executed at Pune on 14 March 2020**

**Promoter:** Skyline Habitat Developers Private Limited, Baner, Pune ("the Promoter")
**Allottee:** Ms Ananya Kulkarni, Kothrud, Pune ("the Allottee")

**Project:** Skyline Serene, Tower B, Baner, Pune
**MahaRERA Registration No.:** P52100099999 (fictional)
**Apartment:** B-1104, carpet area 68.20 sq m, with one covered parking space
**Total consideration:** INR 82,00,000 (Rupees Eighty-Two Lakh), inclusive of GST

**Clause 7.1 – Possession.** The Promoter shall hand over possession of the Apartment to the Allottee on or before **31 December 2022**, along with the occupancy certificate.

**Clause 7.2 – Force majeure.** The date in clause 7.1 shall be extended for any period during which completion is prevented by war, flood, drought, fire, cyclone, earthquake or any other calamity caused by nature, or by any order of a court or authority.

**Clause 7.4 – Delay.** If the Promoter fails to give possession by the date in clause 7.1, the Allottee may, at her option, (a) withdraw from the Project and receive a refund of all amounts paid with interest at the rate prescribed under the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016 and the Maharashtra rules, or (b) continue and receive interest for every month of delay at the same rate, until possession.

**Clause 9 – Payment plan.** Construction-linked, per Schedule C. Time is of the essence for the Allottee's payments.

**Clause 18 – Disputes.** Disputes shall first be attempted to be settled amicably. Failing that, the parties may approach the Real Estate Regulatory Authority, Maharashtra, under the Act.

*Signed by the Promoter's authorised signatory and the Allottee. Registered with the Sub-Registrar, Haveli No. 11, Document No. 4521/2020 (fictional).*
