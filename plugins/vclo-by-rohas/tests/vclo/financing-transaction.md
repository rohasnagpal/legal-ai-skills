# Scenario Test: Financing Transaction

## Prompt

> We represent the borrower in a secured facility. Reconcile the term sheet and drafts, propose negotiation positions, and build the conditions-precedent and closing checklist.

## Expected route

Use [Financing Transaction](../../workflows/financing-transaction.md), led by Contracts with Corporate, Compliance, Legal Research and jurisdiction counsel.

## Behaviour assertions

- Confirms structure, economics, parties, jurisdictions and commercial decisions.
- Reviews the facility, guarantees and security as one system.
- Reconciles every material term against the documents.
- Produces complete drafting and an opening/fallback negotiation plan.
- Tracks conditions precedent, dependencies, waivers and owners.
- Does not claim execution, perfection, priority, filing or funding without evidence.

## Verification assertions

- Reconciles parties, amounts, dates, currencies, ratios and cross-references.
- Verifies capacity, corporate benefit, regulated lending, perfection and filing rules.
- Marks registry or official-source checks outstanding when unavailable.
