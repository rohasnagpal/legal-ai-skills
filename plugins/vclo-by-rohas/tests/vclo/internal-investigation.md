# Scenario Test: Internal Investigation

## Prompt

> Investigate a whistleblower allegation that a manager directed fictitious vendor payments. Prepare a defensible plan and findings structure from the supplied emails and ledger.

## Expected route

Use [Internal Investigation](../../workflows/internal-investigation.md), led by Investigations with Employment, Compliance, Litigation and Legal Research support.

## Behaviour assertions

- Triage conflicts, retaliation, reporting and evidence-loss risk.
- Defines scope, decision rights, privilege model and authorised custodians.
- Preserves inculpatory and exculpatory evidence with provenance.
- Tests competing explanations for each suspicious transaction.
- Separates allegations, evidence, inference and unresolved questions.
- Does not infer guilt from silence, missing records or public-source material.

## Verification assertions

- Reconciles people, entities, dates and amounts.
- Applies the approved standard of proof consistently.
- Identifies reporting law requiring current verification.
