# Commercial Dispute Lifecycle

## Trigger and objective

Use for a commercial dispute from initial breach or demand through forum selection, proceedings, settlement and enforcement. Produce one evolving strategy that connects claims, evidence, procedure, economics and recovery.

## Required inputs

Represented party, counterparties, contracts, governing law, forum clauses, chronology, breach and loss facts, communications, procedural stage, deadlines, assets, settlement posture and desired outcome. Identify missing operative documents and trigger dates before calculating limitation or prescribing a route.

## Agents and skills

The [Litigation Agent](../agents/litigation-agent.md) leads court work and the [Dispute Resolution Agent](../agents/dispute-resolution-agent.md) leads negotiated or ADR routes. Use the [Contracts Agent](../agents/contracts-agent.md), [Investigations Agent](../agents/investigations-agent.md) and [Legal Research Agent](../agents/legal-research-agent.md) where needed, plus the applicable jurisdiction counsel.

Core skills include [legal-notice-analyser](../skills/legal-notice-analyser/SKILL.md), [demand-notice-drafter](../skills/demand-notice-drafter/SKILL.md), [forum-jurisdiction-analyst](../skills/forum-jurisdiction-analyst/SKILL.md), [limitation-checker](../skills/limitation-checker/SKILL.md), [chronology-builder](../skills/chronology-builder/SKILL.md), [evidence-organizer](../skills/evidence-organizer/SKILL.md), [damages-quantifier](../skills/damages-quantifier/SKILL.md), [litigation-strategy-planner](../skills/litigation-strategy-planner/SKILL.md), [settlement-strategy-planner](../skills/settlement-strategy-planner/SKILL.md) and [recovery-strategy-planner](../skills/recovery-strategy-planner/SKILL.md).

## Helpful capability categories

Use [document sources](../integrations/document-sources.md), [email and calendar](../integrations/email-and-calendar.md), [company registries](../integrations/company-registries.md) and [legal research](../integrations/legal-research.md) only where available and authorised.

## Execution sequence

1. Confirm parties, objectives, governing law, forum, dispute clause, procedural posture and urgent deadlines.
2. Build the document inventory and source-linked chronology; separate established facts, allegations and assumptions.
3. Analyse claims, defences, contractual preconditions, jurisdiction, limitation, interim relief and evidence gaps.
4. Quantify supported loss and recovery scenarios, including costs, collectability and counterclaim exposure.
5. Select and sequence demand, negotiation, mediation, arbitration or litigation routes without waiving rights inadvertently.
6. Prepare the chosen notice, pleading or ADR materials and preserve evidence and privilege.
7. Maintain a deadline, evidence, settlement and recovery register as the dispute develops.
8. Plan judgment or award enforcement against verified assets and jurisdictions before treating a merits win as economic recovery.

## Verification

Use [consistency-checker](../skills/consistency-checker/SKILL.md), [assumption-flagger](../skills/assumption-flagger/SKILL.md), [authority-validator](../skills/authority-validator/SKILL.md) and [adversarial-reviewer](../skills/adversarial-reviewer/SKILL.md) proportionately. Verify limitation, forum, service, pre-action and enforcement rules against current primary sources.

## Deliverable

Provide an executive case assessment, chronology, claims-and-evidence matrix, forum and limitation analysis, quantum and collectability view, route strategy, immediate actions, settlement parameters, enforcement plan, information gaps and verification status.

## Fallback behaviour

Without integrations, work from supplied materials and issue a precise retrieval list. Without confirmed law, forum or trigger dates, provide scenarios rather than a final deadline or filing-ready document. Never convert allegations into facts or assume assets exist.
