# Litigation Preparation

## Trigger and objective

Use to prepare or reassess a civil or commercial dispute. Build a defensible, source-linked case view covering chronology, pleadings, evidence, law, limitation, quantum, witnesses and strategy.

## Required inputs

Represented party, jurisdiction and forum, procedural stage, operative pleadings and orders, known deadlines, available evidence and desired relief. Optional inputs include witness accounts, damages material, correspondence, prior advice and settlement posture.

## Agents and skills

The [Litigation Agent](../agents/litigation-agent.md) leads with the [Legal Research Agent](../agents/legal-research-agent.md). Use the [Investigations Agent](../agents/investigations-agent.md) where authenticity, preservation or fact-finding is material.

Core skills are [chronology-builder](../skills/chronology-builder/SKILL.md), [pleadings-analyst](../skills/pleadings-analyst/SKILL.md), [evidence-organizer](../skills/evidence-organizer/SKILL.md), [case-law-analyst](../skills/case-law-analyst/SKILL.md), [limitation-checker](../skills/limitation-checker/SKILL.md), [damages-quantifier](../skills/damages-quantifier/SKILL.md), [cross-examination-planner](../skills/cross-examination-planner/SKILL.md) and [litigation-strategy-planner](../skills/litigation-strategy-planner/SKILL.md).

## Helpful capability categories

[Document sources](../integrations/document-sources.md) may retrieve the case file. [Email and calendar](../integrations/email-and-calendar.md) may support authorised correspondence searches and deadline review. [Legal research](../integrations/legal-research.md) should support current procedural and substantive propositions.

## Execution sequence

1. Freeze the procedural posture, operative documents, forum and deadlines.
2. Build a chronology with exact source locators and confidence states.
3. Map each pleaded element, admission, denial and relief to evidence and missing proof.
4. Analyse preservation, authenticity, admissibility and disclosure issues.
5. Calculate limitation using supported dates and alternative scenarios where facts or law are disputed.
6. Build damages by head and identify causation, mitigation and proof gaps.
7. Analyse witnesses and conflicting accounts without selecting a truth unsupported by evidence.
8. Research controlling law and contrary authority.
9. Develop primary and alternative case theories, procedural options, settlement leverage and a sequenced action plan.

Chronology, pleadings/evidence, law and damages workstreams may run in parallel after a common source inventory is fixed.

## Verification and consolidation

Reconcile names, dates, figures and positions using [consistency-checker](../skills/consistency-checker/SKILL.md). Use [assumption-flagger](../skills/assumption-flagger/SKILL.md), [authority-validator](../skills/authority-validator/SKILL.md), [citation-integrity-checker](../skills/citation-integrity-checker/SKILL.md) and, for major matters, [adversarial-reviewer](../skills/adversarial-reviewer/SKILL.md).

## Deliverable

Provide procedural status, executive case assessment, chronology, issues and evidence matrix, limitation and damages analysis, witness issues, legal authorities, strategy, immediate deadlines, information gaps and verification status.

## Fallback behaviour

Without subagents, run each workstream sequentially. Without integrations, work from supplied evidence. If controlling materials or critical evidence are unavailable, state the resulting limit on the opinion and provide a precise retrieval list. Never convert an allegation into an established fact.
