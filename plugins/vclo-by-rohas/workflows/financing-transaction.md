# Financing Transaction

## Trigger and objective

Use for a bilateral or syndicated loan, secured financing or comparable debt transaction from term sheet through documentation, conditions precedent, signing, perfection and closing. Produce one lender- or borrower-side transaction position and closing record.

## Required inputs

Represented party, borrower and obligors, lender group, jurisdiction, facility type and amount, purpose, term sheet, structure chart, proposed security and guarantees, financial and commercial terms, timetable and available drafts. Confirm regulated-lending and cross-border facts before applying local rules.

## Execution sequence

1. Confirm side represented, structure, economics, entities, governing laws, currencies, purpose and timetable.
2. Reconcile the term sheet, approvals and draft documents; identify open commercial decisions rather than inventing them.
3. Review pricing, drawdown, repayment, representations, undertakings, information covenants, events of default, acceleration, indemnities, tax, transfer and agency provisions as one system.
4. Analyse guarantees and security for capacity, corporate benefit, coverage, priority, perfection, registration and enforcement.
5. Build a conditions-precedent and closing checklist with document owner, form, status, dependency and waiver authority.
6. Prepare complete drafting and negotiation positions, including consequential changes across finance documents.
7. Verify authority, signatures, filings, registrations, fees, taxes and post-closing actions in each relevant jurisdiction.
8. Produce a final documents-versus-terms reconciliation and unresolved-risk register before closing.

## Verification

Run consistency checks across parties, definitions, amounts, currencies, dates, ratios, security scope and cross-references. Verify regulated-lending, financial-assistance, corporate-benefit, guarantee, security, perfection, priority, tax and filing propositions against current local authority.

## Deliverable

Provide an executive deal summary, structure and document map, priority issues, proposed drafting, negotiation plan, conditions-precedent checklist, authority and perfection matrix, closing actions, information gaps and verification status.

## Fallback behaviour

Without complete drafts or the term sheet, provide a gap list and provisional issue framework rather than a closing opinion. Without official registry or filing access, mark perfection and status checks outstanding. Never claim execution, filing, registration, priority or funding has occurred without evidence.
