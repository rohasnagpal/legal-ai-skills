# Internal Investigation

## Trigger and objective

Use for whistleblower reports, suspected misconduct, fraud, conflicts, policy breaches or other internal fact-finding. Produce a fair, lawful and source-linked investigation record that separates evidence, allegation and inference.

## Required inputs

Allegation and source, commissioning authority, entities and jurisdictions, initial scope, immediate safety or retaliation concerns, implicated persons or functions, known evidence, reporting line and required timetable. Optional inputs include policies, prior complaints, systems, transactions and regulator or auditor involvement.

## Execution sequence

1. Triage immediate harm, conflicts, independence, retaliation, reporting obligations and evidence-loss risk.
2. Approve scope, terms of reference, privilege model, decision rights and communications protocol.
3. Issue proportionate preservation steps and create an evidence inventory and custody record.
4. Build allegation, issue, witness and source matrices.
5. Collect and review within authorised custodians, systems, date ranges and search terms.
6. Develop competing hypotheses and test each against inculpatory and exculpatory evidence.
7. Sequence interviews to preserve evidence integrity and fairness; distinguish first-hand knowledge from hearsay or inference.
8. Draft findings using an approved standard of proof and identify confidence, contradictory evidence and unresolved issues.
9. Recommend remediation, control improvement, employment or reporting decisions to authorised decision-makers.

Evidence review, transaction analysis, legal research and control review may run in parallel after scope and preservation are fixed.

## Verification

Use [consistency-checker](../skills/consistency-checker/SKILL.md), [assumption-flagger](../skills/assumption-flagger/SKILL.md) and, for significant matters, [adversarial-reviewer](../skills/adversarial-reviewer/SKILL.md). Verify authority for material reporting or procedural propositions.

## Deliverable

Provide mandate and scope, methodology, allegation matrix, chronology, evidence inventory, findings with exact sources and confidence, contrary evidence, control issues, recommendations, unresolved matters and verification status. Separate privileged advice where required.

## Fallback behaviour

Without subagents, execute the workstreams sequentially. Without connected sources, obtain authorised exports or uploaded files and document collection limits. Do not bypass controls, broaden custodians silently, infer guilt from missing evidence or treat public-source material as authenticated internal evidence.
