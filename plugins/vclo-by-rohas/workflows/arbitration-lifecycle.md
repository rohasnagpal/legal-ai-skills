# Arbitration Lifecycle

## Trigger and objective

Use for an arbitration from clause assessment and notice through tribunal constitution, interim relief, pleadings, evidence, hearing, award, challenge and enforcement. Maintain a coherent procedural and merits strategy across the lifecycle.

## Required inputs

Represented party, contract and arbitration clause, amendments, seat, venue, governing law, rules, institution, dispute facts, notices, procedural stage, deadlines, relief, counterparty location and known assets. Do not infer the seat from the hearing venue.

## Execution sequence

1. Confirm the arbitration agreement, parties, scope, seat, rules, institution, governing law and mandatory law.
2. Test conditions precedent, limitation, notice requirements, consolidation, joinder and parallel-proceeding risks.
3. Frame claims, defences, counterclaims, relief and evidence; issue a compliant notice without overcommitting the case.
4. Address tribunal constitution, independence, challenge and emergency or court interim relief.
5. Build the procedural timetable, document-production plan, witness and expert strategy and costs record.
6. Draft and reconcile pleadings, evidence and hearing themes against the burden of proof and requested relief.
7. Analyse the award, correction or interpretation options, challenge deadlines and resistance grounds.
8. Plan recognition and enforcement in asset jurisdictions, distinguishing award status from actual recovery.

## Verification

Verify the current institutional rules, lex arbitri, mandatory seat law, limitation, court-support powers, challenge route and enforcement convention. Use assumption, consistency, authority, citation and adversarial checks proportionately.

## Deliverable

Provide a lifecycle plan, clause and jurisdiction analysis, deadline register, claims-and-evidence matrix, procedural strategy, interim-relief position, hearing plan, award options, enforcement route, information gaps and verification status.

## Fallback behaviour

Without a complete clause, applicable rules or confirmed seat, do not issue a final procedural opinion. Without live authority, mark seat-law and enforcement propositions unverified. Do not claim institutional filing, arbitrator appointment or court relief has occurred unless confirmed.
