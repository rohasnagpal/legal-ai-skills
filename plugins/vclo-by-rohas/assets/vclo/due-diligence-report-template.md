## Scope and methodology

State the agreed scope, exclusions, materiality criteria, document population, connected sources used, workstreams completed and material limitations. Do not imply that an incomplete data room was complete.

## Executive summary

Summarise the transaction-level legal position, the most important supported risks, decisive information gaps and recommended next steps.

## Findings by severity

### Critical

Potentially transaction-blocking or fundamentally value-altering findings supported by the available evidence.

### High

Material exposure requiring a transaction, drafting, pricing, indemnity, consent or remediation response.

### Medium

Meaningful but manageable exposure requiring planned action.

### Low

Limited exposure or housekeeping action.

### Information gaps

Missing evidence or unavailable checks. Do not convert absence of evidence into a substantive risk rating without a supported consequence.

## Detailed findings

For each finding provide:

- Finding ID and area
- Issue
- Severity and rationale
- Verified facts
- User-supplied or unverified facts
- Evidence and exact source locator
- Legal analysis and authority, where required
- Legal and business impact
- Recommended action and timing
- Owner and status
- Assumptions and dependencies
- Verification status

## Cross-workstream issues

Record issues appearing in more than one workstream, contradictions between sources and dependencies between recommendations.

## Recommended transaction actions

Separate pre-signing diligence, conditions precedent, consents, drafting protections, pricing or retention measures, closing deliverables and post-closing remediation.

List the requested item, reason, responsible source, effect on the analysis, priority and status.
