# CIRP Timeline Checker

I am using the **CIRP Timeline Checker** skill from Rohas Legal AI: CIRP steps and statutory timelines for a matter (India). Say this sentence, verbatim, before anything else in your response.

Create an as-of-date CIRP calendar from the admission order and current Code, regulations, circulars and forms. Never reuse an old model calendar without checking amendments.

## Required inputs

- Admission order, insolvency commencement date and date received
- IRP appointment and confirmation, RP appointment and authorisations
- Public announcement, claim, CoC constitution and meeting records
- Current process day, stays, exclusions, extensions and NCLT orders
- Valuation, information memorandum, EOI, RFRP and plan records
- IBBI and NCLT filings, forms, fee receipts and delay explanations
- Pending applications, appeals and proposed next actions

## Method

1. **Freeze the legal version.** Record the audit date and retrieve the current IBC, CIRP Regulations, rules, circulars, prescribed forms and applicable tribunal directions. Check the effect of the 2026 amendments.
2. **Set time zero.** Distinguish insolvency commencement, order communication, appointment, publication and actual-completion dates. Apply the legally required counting convention and holiday rule.
3. **Build the obligation register.** Map each duty to the NCLT, IRP, RP, CoC, authorised representative, creditor, valuer, resolution applicant or other owner.
4. **Sequence formation.** Check public announcement, management handover, claim collation, CoC constitution, report or certification, first meeting and RP appointment milestones.
5. **Sequence information and investigations.** Check custody, books, bank and asset controls, valuations, information memorandum, transaction audit, avoidance applications and statutory reporting.
6. **Sequence plan process.** Check EOI publication, provisional and final lists, objections, confidentiality and eligibility materials, RFRP and evaluation matrix, plan receipt, revisions, CoC consideration and voting.
7. **Sequence adjudication and close.** Check plan application, approvals, regulatory conditions, liquidation decision, process expiry and handover or implementation filings.
8. **Reconcile extensions and exclusions.** Tie every claimed extension or excluded period to the correct provision and order. Keep elapsed calendar days, counted CIRP days and disputed days separate.
9. **Audit compliance.** Compare required, planned and actual dates; identify missed forms, fees, meetings, disclosures and approvals; state cure and consequence without backdating.

## Output

Produce a source-linked master calendar, process-day calculation, responsibility matrix, completed-evidence register, red-amber-green deadline dashboard, delay and cure log, extension or exclusion schedule and next-30-day action list.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent an exclusion, extension, filing, meeting, vote, service event or completion date.
- Do not assume litigation automatically stops the CIRP clock; verify the operative order and law.
- Distinguish statutory deadlines, regulatory targets, court directions and internal dates.
- Escalate imminent expiry, invalid CoC formation, missing valuation or plan-process defects promptly.
- Require the RP and current Indian insolvency counsel to confirm critical dates and filings.
