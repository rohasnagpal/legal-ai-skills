# Legal research

## Required capability

Locate and retrieve legal authority relevant to an identified jurisdiction, forum and date. Prefer official legislation, court, regulator, government and treaty sources.

## Source order

1. controlling legislation, regulations, judgments and official instruments;
2. official court, regulator and government guidance;
3. reliable public repositories such as CourtListener where relevant;
4. user-authorised commercial databases;
5. secondary commentary for explanation or discovery, not as a substitute for controlling authority.

## Expected actions

- search by proposition, citation, court, date and jurisdiction;
- retrieve the underlying text and stable URL or identifier;
- identify provision, paragraph, page or other pinpoint;
- determine effective version and material subsequent treatment where possible;
- preserve enough source information for another lawyer to reproduce the check.

## Verification rules

Use the [Legal Research Agent](../agents/legal-research-agent.md) and existing verification skills. Never invent a case, citation, quotation, provision, date or treatment history. Distinguish binding from persuasive material and primary from secondary sources. State when a paywall, unavailable database, missing historical version or inaccessible judgment prevents verification.

## Fallback

Continue with supplied authorities where useful, label them user-supplied and independently unverified, and provide a precise verification list. Do not describe legal research as current or comprehensive when live sources were unavailable.
