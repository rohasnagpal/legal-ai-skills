# United Kingdom legal sources

Last reviewed: 20 September 2026.

Identify whether the matter concerns England and Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland, UK-wide legislation, a devolved matter or UK Supreme Court jurisdiction. Do not blend their substantive law or procedure.

## Legislation — official

- **[legislation.gov.uk](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/)** — UK Public General Acts, statutory instruments, devolved legislation and historical or revised versions where available.

Check the site's status indicators, commencement provisions, amendment history and outstanding effects. A revised text may not yet incorporate every recent change.

## Courts and tribunals — official

- **[The National Archives — Find Case Law](https://caselaw.nationalarchives.gov.uk/)** — official judgments and tribunal decisions, principally England and Wales plus UK-wide Supreme Court and Privy Council material.
- **[UK Supreme Court cases](https://www.supremecourt.uk/cases)** — case information, judgments and linked materials.
- **[Courts and Tribunals Judiciary](https://www.judiciary.uk/)** — England and Wales judgments, rules, practice directions and judicial material.
- **[Scottish Courts and Tribunals Service](https://www.scotcourts.gov.uk/)** — Scotland court and tribunal material.
- **[Judiciary NI](https://www.judiciaryni.uk/)** — Northern Ireland judgments and judiciary material.
- **Relevant tribunal or regulator website** — use the official body governing the issue and distinguish decisions, rules and guidance.

## Reputable free discovery source

- **[BAILII](https://www.bailii.org/)** — useful for cross-jurisdictional case searching, historical judgments and tribunal material. Use an official court or National Archives copy for final verification where reasonably available.

## United Kingdom-specific checks

- State the relevant legal jurisdiction expressly: England and Wales, Scotland or Northern Ireland.
- Record the neutral citation, court, date, disposition, paragraph pinpoints and later appellate history.
- Check whether legislation extends to the relevant territory and was commenced there on the material date.
- Distinguish binding authority from persuasive decisions across UK jurisdictions and court levels.
- Distinguish a press summary or case summary from the judgment itself.
- Check applicable Civil Procedure Rules, tribunal rules, Scottish rules or Northern Ireland procedure from the relevant official source rather than assuming a UK-wide rule.

## Preferred sequence

`legislation.gov.uk / National Archives Find Case Law / official court, tribunal or regulator → BAILII for discovery and older material → secondary commentary`
