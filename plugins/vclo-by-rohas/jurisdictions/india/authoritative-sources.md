# India legal sources

Last reviewed: 24 September 2026.

Use this guide only after confirming that Indian law applies and identifying the relevant State, court, tribunal or authority. Central legislation alone may not answer a question involving State law, local rules or court-specific procedure.

## Official primary sources

- **[India Code](https://indiacode.gov.in/)** — Central Acts and subordinate legislation, and State material where available. The former `indiacode.nic.in` site now redirects here.
- **[eGazette of India](https://egazette.gov.in/)** — notifications, commencement material, amendments, rules and official publication history.
- **[Supreme Court of India](https://www.sci.gov.in/)** — judgments, orders, rules, case information and official court material.
- **[Supreme Court Reports search](https://scr.sci.gov.in/scrsearch/)** — Supreme Court judgments and reported authorities searchable by case, citation, statute or provision.
- **[Supreme Court Rules](https://www.sci.gov.in/supreme-court-rules/)** — current rules and amendment notifications for Supreme Court practice.
- **[eCourts e-Filing](https://filing.ecourts.gov.in/pdedev/)** — filing facilities adopted by participating High Courts and district courts; availability does not replace the relevant court's current rules, scrutiny requirements or local directions.
- **[eCourts Services](https://services.ecourts.gov.in/)** — case status, orders, judgments and linked court services for participating courts; confirm identity and completeness against the relevant court record.
- **Relevant High Court, tribunal and district judiciary website** — use the official site with territorial or subject-matter jurisdiction. Confirm the court and document identity before relying on a downloaded copy.

## Official regulators and government sources

Use the regulator or department governing the issue, including where relevant:

- [Reserve Bank of India](https://www.rbi.org.in/)
- [Securities and Exchange Board of India](https://www.sebi.gov.in/)
- [Competition Commission of India](https://www.cci.gov.in/)
- [Ministry of Corporate Affairs](https://www.mca.gov.in/)
- [Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology](https://www.meity.gov.in/)
- [Income Tax Department](https://www.incometax.gov.in/)
- [Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs](https://www.cbic.gov.in/)

Treat circulars, FAQs, press releases and guidance according to their actual legal status; do not equate explanatory material with legislation or binding directions.

## Reputable free discovery source

- **[Indian Kanoon](https://indiankanoon.org/)** — useful for full-text searching, related authorities and locating judgments. It is a discovery source, not a substitute for the official statutory text or an official judgment where one is reasonably available.

## India-specific checks

- Verify commencement and the version in force on the legally relevant date through the Act, amending instrument and Gazette material.
- Identify Central versus State law and any State rules, amendments, notifications or court rules.
- For cases, record the court, bench, date, citation, disposition and later treatment; distinguish binding Supreme Court authority from the relevant High Court and persuasive decisions.
- Check whether a judgment copy is complete, reportable, corrected, stayed, reviewed or affected by appeal.
- Do not infer nationwide procedure from one High Court's practice.
- For filing work, verify the current High Court or court rules, practice directions, roster or jurisdiction, prescribed form, court fee, e-filing adoption, PDF and signature requirements, affidavit, annexures, scrutiny objections and service process from the relevant official court sources.

## Preferred sequence

`India Code / eGazette / official court or tribunal / official regulator → Indian Kanoon for discovery and cross-searching → secondary commentary`
