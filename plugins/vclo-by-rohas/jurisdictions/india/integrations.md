# India integrations and registries

Use only sources and connectors that are actually available and authorised for the matter.

## Legal research

Follow the [authoritative-source guide](authoritative-sources.md). Browser or research-tool availability must be confirmed at runtime. If current primary law cannot be retrieved, mark the affected proposition **UNVERIFIED — LIVE LEGAL RESEARCH UNAVAILABLE**.

India Code, eGazette, Supreme Court, High Court, tribunal, regulator and eCourts services are authoritative or official research destinations, not bundled unrestricted APIs. Use them through available browsing or an authorised research tool, respect access controls and never imply that a site was programmatically queried when it was not.

## Corporate and regulatory records

The Ministry of Corporate Affairs, regulators, courts, tribunals and State authorities may provide public search facilities, filings or cause-list information. Coverage, authentication, account requirements and access conditions vary. Do not claim that vCLO has queried one unless the relevant connector or browser access was actually available and used.

## Courts and filing

- Use the relevant Supreme Court, High Court, tribunal or district judiciary website for current rules, practice directions, forms, fees, rosters, filing notices and judgments.
- eCourts Services and e-Filing may assist with participating High Courts and district courts, but adoption and available functions vary by court and State.
- Treat filing portals as operational systems, not authority for substantive law. Check the governing rule or official direction separately.
- Do not upload, file, pay, sign or serve through a court system without explicit authority and the required advocate or litigant credentials.

## User-authorised systems

Documents, email, calendars, cloud storage, repositories and commercial legal databases may be used only within the user's authorised scope. Preserve confidentiality and provenance. Never place credentials in a prompt, skill, repository file or output.

## No-result rule

An empty registry, court or regulator search is not proof that an entity, filing, proceeding, licence, charge or order does not exist. Record the source, search terms, date, coverage and access limitation.
