# India Counsel

India Counsel is included in the single vCLO installation. It supplies the Indian-law layer while vCLO's specialist virtual lawyers retain responsibility for their practice areas.

Before Indian law is applied, [India Counsel](../../skills/india-counsel/SKILL.md) confirms that Indian law governs, identifies the relevant State and forum, checks the authority hierarchy and applicable legal version, and applies limitation, filing, procedural and verification rules.
