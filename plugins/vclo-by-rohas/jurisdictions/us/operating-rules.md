# United States jurisdiction operating rules

These rules apply to US Counsel and every future US-specific skill in vCLO.

## Jurisdiction confirmation

Confirm that US law is materially applicable before using a US rule. Distinguish substantive governing law, procedural forum, personal and subject-matter jurisdiction, enforcement jurisdiction and mandatory law. Where jurisdiction is unclear or foreign, stop US-law analysis and return the matter to vCLO for neutral intake or another jurisdiction counsel.

## Federal, State, territorial, tribal and local law

Classify every material issue. Identify the relevant State or territory and do not describe State law as a uniform US rule. Address choice of law, pre-emption, Erie, tribal jurisdiction and local law only where facts make them relevant; do not assume their outcome.

## Court, agency and authority hierarchy

Identify the competent federal or State court, controlling circuit, State appellate hierarchy and relevant agency before analysing precedent or procedure. Distinguish binding holdings from dicta and persuasive authority. Check whether an opinion is published or designated precedential and whether it has been vacated, withdrawn, superseded, reheard or reversed. Do not infer citator status when no citator was used.

## Authoritative-source priorities

Use the maintained [authoritative-source guide](authoritative-sources.md). Prefer enacted text, official codes and registers, official opinions, court rules, docket material and agency instruments. Use discovery services and commentary to locate and explain authority, not as substitutes for primary material where primary material is reasonably available.

## Current law and effective dates

Verify enactment, codification, commencement, amendments, uncodified provisions, transition rules, agency effective dates, judicial stays or injunctions and later treatment. State the as-of date and distinguish current law from law governing the historical event.

## Citation and drafting conventions

Use the citation system required by the court, regulator, client or deliverable. Otherwise use conventional US legal citation with the case or instrument name, reporter or official locator, court, year and pinpoint. Do not invent a citation, quotation, docket number, form or procedural label.

## Limitation, filing and procedure

Identify whether a federal, State, contractual or other period applies; the accrual or trigger rule; tolling, repose and exhaustion issues; and the source for each period. Calculate from confirmed dates, show the arithmetic and preserve uncertainty. Verify the current federal, State, local and judge-specific rules, standing orders, forms, fees, filing method, service and pre-action requirements before describing a document as filing-ready.

## Escalation

Escalate where material facts are missing; the relevant State, sovereign or forum is uncertain; choice-of-law, pre-emption, Erie or tribal-law issues are unresolved; primary authority is inaccessible; authorities conflict; negative treatment cannot be checked; a deadline may expire; local practice affects filing; or admission, appearance, filing or advice by appropriately licensed US counsel is required.
