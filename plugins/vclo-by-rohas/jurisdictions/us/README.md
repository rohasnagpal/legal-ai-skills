# US Counsel

US Counsel is included in the single vCLO installation. It supplies the United States federal, State, territorial, tribal and local-law layer while vCLO's specialist virtual lawyers retain responsibility for their practice areas.

Before US law is applied, [US Counsel](../../skills/us-counsel/SKILL.md) confirms that US law governs, separates federal and State issues, identifies the relevant State and forum, checks controlling authority and effective dates, and applies limitation, filing, procedural and verification rules.

## MVP skills

- [us-federal-state-issue-mapper](../../skills/us-federal-state-issue-mapper/SKILL.md) — separates sovereign, governing-law and forum issues
- [us-state-law-research-planner](../../skills/us-state-law-research-planner/SKILL.md) — official-source-first State-law research plans
- [us-federal-civil-procedure-checker](../../skills/us-federal-civil-procedure-checker/SKILL.md) — federal civil procedure and court-specific checks
- [us-sec-reporting-checker](../../skills/us-sec-reporting-checker/SKILL.md) — SEC reporting and filing applicability
- [us-employment-law-applicability-checker](../../skills/us-employment-law-applicability-checker/SKILL.md) — federal, State and local employment coverage
- [us-privacy-law-applicability-checker](../../skills/us-privacy-law-applicability-checker/SKILL.md) — federal, State and sectoral privacy coverage

These scoped skills supplement vCLO's jurisdiction-neutral library. They do not imply comprehensive coverage of every State, territory or tribal jurisdiction.
