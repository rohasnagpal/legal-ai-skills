# United Kingdom authoritative sources

Last reviewed: 24 September 2026.

Identify whether the matter concerns England and Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland, UK-wide legislation, a devolved matter or UK Supreme Court jurisdiction. Do not blend their substantive law or procedure.

## Legislation — official

- **[legislation.gov.uk](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/)** — UK Public General Acts, statutory instruments, devolved legislation and historical or revised versions where available. vCLO includes bounded read-only search and retrieval tools.
- **Relevant legislature** — [UK Parliament](https://www.parliament.uk/), [Scottish Parliament](https://www.parliament.scot/), [Senedd Cymru](https://senedd.wales/) or [Northern Ireland Assembly](https://www.niassembly.gov.uk/) for bills, legislative history and official parliamentary material.
- **Relevant government or regulator** — commencement orders, statutory guidance, rules and official instruments within that body's remit.

Check territorial extent, application, commencement provisions, amendment history and outstanding effects. A revised text may not yet incorporate every recent change.

## Courts and tribunals — official

- **[The National Archives — Find Case Law](https://caselaw.nationalarchives.gov.uk/)** — official judgments and tribunal decisions, principally England and Wales plus UK-wide Supreme Court and Privy Council material. vCLO includes bounded read-only search and retrieval tools subject to the service's reasonable-use and licensing terms.
- **[UK Supreme Court cases](https://www.supremecourt.uk/cases)** — case information, judgments and linked materials.
- **[Courts and Tribunals Judiciary](https://www.judiciary.uk/)** — England and Wales judgments, rules, practice directions and judicial material.
- **[Civil Procedure Rules](https://www.justice.gov.uk/courts/procedure-rules/civil)** — rules and practice directions for civil proceedings in England and Wales; verify the current update and any court-specific requirements.
- **[Scottish Courts and Tribunals Service](https://www.scotcourts.gov.uk/)** — Scottish judgments, court rules, practice material, forms and fees.
- **[Judiciary NI](https://www.judiciaryni.uk/)** and **[Department of Justice NI court rules](https://www.justice-ni.gov.uk/topics/courts-and-tribunals/court-rules)** — Northern Ireland judgments, rules and judiciary material.
- **Relevant tribunal or regulator website** — use the official body governing the issue and distinguish decisions, rules and guidance.

## Regulators and official guidance

Use the responsible official regulator or public body's site, for example the FCA, ICO, CMA or Companies House, only within its remit. Distinguish legislation and binding rules from codes, statutory guidance, manuals, consultations, speeches, FAQs and enforcement summaries.

## Reputable free discovery source

- **[BAILII](https://www.bailii.org/)** — useful for cross-jurisdictional case searching, historical judgments and tribunal material. Use an official court or National Archives copy for final verification where reasonably available.

## Verification requirements

- State the relevant legal jurisdiction expressly: England and Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland or genuinely UK-wide.
- Record the neutral citation, court, date, disposition, paragraph pinpoints and later appellate history.
- Check whether legislation extends and applies to the relevant territory and was commenced there on the material date.
- Check the legislation.gov.uk outstanding-effects warning and relevant commencement or amending instruments.
- Distinguish binding authority from persuasive decisions across UK jurisdictions and court levels.
- Distinguish a press summary, headnote, explanatory note or regulator summary from the controlling text.
- Check the applicable Civil, Criminal, Family, tribunal, Scottish or Northern Ireland procedure and local practice rather than assuming a UK-wide rule.

## Preferred sequence

`legislation.gov.uk / National Archives Find Case Law / official court, tribunal or regulator → BAILII for discovery and older material → secondary commentary`
