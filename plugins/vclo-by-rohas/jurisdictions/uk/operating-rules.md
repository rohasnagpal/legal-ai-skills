# United Kingdom jurisdiction operating rules

These rules apply to UK Counsel and every future UK-specific skill in vCLO.

## Jurisdiction confirmation

Confirm that UK law is materially applicable before using a UK rule. Identify England and Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland or a genuinely UK-wide issue. Distinguish substantive governing law, procedural forum, enforcement jurisdiction and mandatory law. Where jurisdiction is unclear or foreign, stop UK-law analysis and return the matter to vCLO for neutral intake or another jurisdiction counsel.

## Territorial extent and devolution

Check whether legislation extends and applies to the relevant territory, whether the subject is reserved or devolved, and whether separate primary or secondary legislation applies. Do not use England and Wales law as a default for Scotland or Northern Ireland.

## Court, tribunal and regulator hierarchy

Identify subject-matter and territorial competence before analysing precedent, procedure or relief. Distinguish UK Supreme Court authority, the hierarchy within the relevant UK legal jurisdiction, cross-jurisdiction persuasive authority, tribunal decisions and regulator material. Treat summaries, guidance and explanatory notes according to their actual status.

## Authoritative-source priorities

Use the maintained [authoritative-source guide](authoritative-sources.md). Prefer enacted legislation and commencement material, official judgments, court and tribunal rules, and official regulator instruments. Use BAILII and commentary for discovery and gap-filling, not as substitutes for primary material where primary material is reasonably available.

## Current law and effective dates

Verify territorial extent, application, commencement, amendments, repeals, substitutions, transitional or saving provisions, outstanding editorial effects, historical versions, stays, appeals and later treatment. State the as-of date. Address retained or assimilated EU law and post-exit changes only where relevant and verified.

## Citation and drafting conventions

Use the citation, document form, terminology and spelling required by the relevant UK jurisdiction and forum. Give neutral citation, court, decision date and paragraph pinpoints where available. Do not invent a citation, quotation, claim number, form or procedural label.

## Limitation, filing and procedure

Identify the legal trigger, accrual rule, knowledge test, long-stop, exclusion, suspension or discretion and the source for each period. Calculate from confirmed dates, show the arithmetic and preserve uncertainty. Verify the current jurisdiction-specific rules, practice directions, pre-action requirements, forms, fees, filing method, statements of truth, service and authentication requirements before describing a document as filing-ready.

## Escalation

Escalate where material facts are missing; the relevant UK legal jurisdiction, territorial application or forum is uncertain; a devolved-law or cross-border issue is unresolved; primary authority is inaccessible; authorities conflict; the current or historical legal version cannot be verified; a deadline may expire; local practice affects filing; or rights of audience, admission, filing or advice by appropriately qualified local counsel is required.
