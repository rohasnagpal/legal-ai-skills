# UK Counsel

UK Counsel is included in the single vCLO installation. It supplies the law and procedure of England and Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland and genuinely UK-wide matters while vCLO's specialist virtual lawyers retain responsibility for their practice areas.

Before UK law is applied, [UK Counsel](../../skills/uk-counsel/SKILL.md) confirms that UK law governs, identifies the correct UK legal jurisdiction and forum, checks territorial extent, devolution, authority hierarchy and effective dates, and applies limitation, filing, procedural and verification rules.

## MVP skills

- [uk-companies-act-compliance-checker](../../skills/uk-companies-act-compliance-checker/SKILL.md) — company records, approvals and filings
- [uk-data-protection-compliance-checker](../../skills/uk-data-protection-compliance-checker/SKILL.md) — UK GDPR, DPA 2018 and PECR compliance
- [uk-employment-law-applicability-checker](../../skills/uk-employment-law-applicability-checker/SKILL.md) — employment coverage across UK jurisdictions
- [england-wales-pre-action-protocol-checker](../../skills/england-wales-pre-action-protocol-checker/SKILL.md) — protocol and Practice Direction compliance
- [england-wales-civil-claim-drafter](../../skills/england-wales-civil-claim-drafter/SKILL.md) — claim form and particulars framework
- [uk-insolvency-applicability-checker](../../skills/uk-insolvency-applicability-checker/SKILL.md) — corporate insolvency and restructuring routes

These scoped skills supplement vCLO's jurisdiction-neutral library. England and Wales procedure is not applied to Scotland or Northern Ireland.
