# Verification Status

Apply a status to each material factual or legal proposition where verification matters.

| Status | Meaning | Minimum record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Verified against supplied evidence | The proposition is supported by identified material in the supplied record. | Document and exact locator |
| Verified against primary authority | The legal proposition was checked against the applicable primary source. | Authority, provision or paragraph, version/date and source |
| Secondary-source only | A secondary source supports the proposition, but the underlying primary authority was not verified. | Source and reason primary verification was unavailable |
| User-supplied but not independently verified | The user provided the fact or proposition and no independent check was completed. | Instruction or source description |
| Unresolved | Available sources conflict or are insufficient to determine the proposition. | Conflicting material and required next step |
| Unavailable source | A required source or connected capability could not be accessed. | Source sought, access limitation and effect |
