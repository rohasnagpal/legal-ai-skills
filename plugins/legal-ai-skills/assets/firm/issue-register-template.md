## Supporting fields

Where needed, add below the table or in the matter system:

- severity rationale;
- source verification status;
- represented party and jurisdiction;
- assumption or dependency;
- target completion date;
- linked finding or request-list item;
- resolution evidence and approver.

Do not mark an item resolved without recording the evidence supporting closure.
