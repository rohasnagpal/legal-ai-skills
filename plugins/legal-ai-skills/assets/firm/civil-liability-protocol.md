# Civil Liability Research and Evidence Protocol

Apply this protocol to general and focused civil-liability skills. These are analytical methods, not statements of a universal law of torts.

## Jurisdiction and inputs

Establish represented side, relevant countries and local units, choice of law, forum, relevant dates, conduct, alleged harm, parties, documents and desired relief. Distinguish substantive law, procedure and enforcement. Ask material unanswered questions; preserve the firm's first-contact gate for raw accounts. Do not assume common-law torts apply in a civil-law jurisdiction or that a criminal/regulatory allegation establishes civil liability.

## Sources and method

Use the [research protocol](../../integrations/legal-research.md) and available jurisdiction counsel through the Managing Partner. Verify each material local test, defence, remedy and limitation rule from authoritative text retrieved this session or supplied, recording jurisdiction, pinpoint, version and access date. No remembered case, provision, threshold or deadline may be presented as verified. Without a maintained module, use bounded official-source research and disclose coverage limits.

Map each sourced element and burden to document-supported facts, user statements, allegations, contrary evidence, assumptions and missing proof. Analyse liability for each party and competing explanations. Separate breach, causation and quantum. Legal calculators apply only after the governing rule and trigger are verified. Use existing damages, limitation, product-liability and evidence skills where appropriate; never invent amounts or success percentages.

## Output and fallback

Provide a represented-party assessment, jurisdiction and date scope, cause/element/evidence matrix, strongest contrary case and defences, causation and loss analysis, remedies, verified timing, evidence plan and source/gap register. Source status is verified, unverified or source unavailable; legal uncertainty is separate from missing evidence. Identify precise qualified local or expert verification needed.

If law cannot be checked, provide a provisional issue framework and mark law-dependent conclusions **UNVERIFIED — LIVE LEGAL RESEARCH UNAVAILABLE** where applicable. Do not convert missing sources into merits conclusions. Do not claim exhaustive coverage, expert findings, filings or professional sign-off. Request other lawyers through the Managing Partner; never start them. No sending, filing, payment or external disclosure is authorised merely by analysis.
