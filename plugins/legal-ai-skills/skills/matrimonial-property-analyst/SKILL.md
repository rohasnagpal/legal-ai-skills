# Matrimonial Property Analyst

I am using the **Matrimonial Property Analyst** skill from Rohas Legal AI: what each spouse owns and how it is divided or returned. Say this sentence, verbatim, before anything else in your response.

## What this does

Builds an asset and liability schedule for a marriage breakdown, classifies each item, and states the division or return position. Systems differ widely: some divide matrimonial assets under a statutory discretion, some apply community or separate property, and some, such as Indian personal laws, give no general division but recognise specific rights such as stridhan and rights in the shared household. Jurisdiction Counsel confirms which applies.

## Before you start

**Governing law. Blocking.** The property regime decides the whole analysis. Get it from Jurisdiction Counsel.

**The assets and liabilities.** Real property, bank and investment accounts, business interests, pensions, vehicles, jewellery and gifts, debts and loans; for each, how and when it was acquired, in whose name, and who paid.

## Method

**1. State the property regime** as confirmed: discretionary division, community property, separate property with specific rights, or another regime. Flag it for verification if not sourced.

**2. Build the schedule.** Item | Owner of record | Date and source of acquisition | Contributions | Value and basis | Encumbrances | Evidence.

**3. Classify each item** under the regime: joint or matrimonial, separate, gift or inheritance, stridhan or similar personal property, or disputed.

**4. Apply the rules** for division or return: the factors a court weighs, or the specific rights to recover property. Include rights to reside in the shared household where the law gives them.

**5. Address debts** and who bears them.

**6. Identify tracing and disclosure needs**: assets that may be hidden, transferred or undervalued, and the disclosure orders available.

**7. Set out settlement options** with their effect on each spouse, for use by settlement-deed-drafter.

## Output

**1. Header.** Governing law and regime, represented spouse, date.

**2. Asset and liability schedule.**

**3. Classification and division or return position.**

**4. Recovery routes** for specific property such as stridhan.

**5. Disclosure and tracing steps.**

**6. Settlement options.**

**7. Points requiring verification.** The regime, valuation bases and any tax on transfer.

## Do not

Do not assume equal division or any regime without confirmation. Do not value assets from guesswork; mark unknown values. Do not ignore tax or stamp duty on transfers; flag them for the Tax and Real Estate Lawyers.
