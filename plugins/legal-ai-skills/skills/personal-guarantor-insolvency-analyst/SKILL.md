## What this does

Explains and analyses an insolvency resolution process against a personal guarantor of a corporate debtor, for either the creditor or the guarantor: initiation, the interim moratorium, the resolution professional's report, the repayment plan, and bankruptcy if the plan fails. It also tests the guarantor's defences on the guarantee itself.

## Before you start

**The client's role. Blocking.** Creditor or guarantor.

**The guarantee and the debt**: the deed of guarantee, the underlying facility, the demand or invocation, and the status of any proceedings against the corporate debtor.

**Law as in force.** India Counsel confirms the provisions, rules and forum that apply.

## Method

**1. Initiation**: who can apply, the preconditions such as demand and default, and the forum, flagged for verification.

**2. Interim moratorium**: when it starts, what it stays, and its effect on other proceedings against the guarantor.

**3. Guarantee defences**: validity and scope of the guarantee, invocation, discharge by variation or release, limitation, and the effect of the corporate debtor's resolution plan on the guarantor's liability.

**4. Process**: resolution professional's report, admission or rejection, claims, the repayment plan, creditor meeting and approval, and implementation.

**5. Bankruptcy**: when it follows, and its consequences for the guarantor's assets and disabilities.

**6. Strategy** for the client's side, with deadlines.

## Output

**1. Header.** Creditor, guarantor, corporate debtor, guarantee, stage.

**2. Initiation and moratorium analysis.**

**3. Guarantee defences.**

**4. Process map and deadlines.**

**5. Strategy.**

**6. Points requiring verification.**

## Do not

Do not assume the corporate debtor's resolution discharges the guarantor; check the current law and the guarantee terms. Do not state forum or thresholds from memory without flagging them.
