# Canadian Public Law Remedies Checker

Read and apply the [Canada Counsel instructions](../../agents/canada-counsel.md) and [source guide](../../jurisdictions/canada/authoritative-sources.md) before substantive work.

## Jurisdiction gate

Confirm the province or territory and federal nexus; distinguish Quebec civil law from common-law private law elsewhere, English/French authoritative texts and Indigenous legal interests. Never infer provincial law from a federal source.

If the matter is governed by another jurisdiction, or the governing jurisdiction is unclear, do not apply Canadian rules. Return it to the Managing Partner for neutral intake or the appropriate counsel. Distinguish substantive law, procedural forum, seat and enforcement.

## Inputs

Establish represented party, intended use, relevant documents and versions, procedural stage, material dates and available source access. Ask for missing facts only where they affect the conclusion.

## Method

Identify decision, legal power, reasons, standing and relief. Distinguish statutory appeal, merits review, judicial review and constitutional challenge; verify exhaustion, urgency and time limits.

### Local applicability checks

Identify federal versus provincial decision-maker and judicial-review court, statutory appeal and Charter issues; verify exhaustion and notice/service requirements.

Use the [operating rules](../../jurisdictions/canada/operating-rules.md). For each material proposition verify primary text, provision/paragraph, legal status, version and effective date, official URL and retrieval date. Distinguish verified law from assumptions and unavailable checks. Check reforms, transitions and adverse authority at execution time.

## Output

Deliver usable findings or drafting with document locators, legal reasons, practical actions, owners, evidence gaps and verification status. Follow [matter continuity](../../assets/firm/matter-continuity.md) for document coverage and deadline records. Use neutral practice skills for the deliverable and this skill for the local-law layer.

## Safeguards

Do not import another country's rules, invent authorities, thresholds, rates or periods, or imply paid registry/private docket access. Verify legal premises before calculations. Filing readiness requires current forum, forms, fees, service and representation checks. Request other lawyers through the Managing Partner; respect scope and confidentiality.
