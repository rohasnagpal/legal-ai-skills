# Misrepresentation and Deceit Analyst

## Purpose and scope

Assess civil claims arising from misleading statements or omissions, reliance and loss without presuming fraud.

## Required inputs and jurisdiction gate

Read and apply the [Civil Liability Research and Evidence Protocol](../../assets/firm/civil-liability-protocol.md), including its inputs, jurisdiction gate, research, output and fallback requirements. The Litigation Lawyer owns this analysis; relevant jurisdiction counsel supplies local-law support. Use the local civil-liability framework rather than importing a foreign tort test.

## Method

1. Obtain the exact statement or omission, speaker, audience, context, dates, source documents, transaction and alleged reliance. Distinguish factual allegations from evidence of dishonesty.
2. Verify available fraudulent, negligent, innocent, statutory or other local misrepresentation routes and their distinct elements. Silence, predictions and opinion require locally sourced treatment.
3. Map falsity, knowledge or recklessness where required, duty for omissions or negligent statements where relevant, intended audience, actual reliance, inducement and causation to evidence for each route.
4. Test alternative explanations, reasonable reliance where required, disclaimers, non-reliance terms and statutory restrictions; do not assume a contractual clause extinguishes all claims.
5. Assess rescission, damages, affirmation, restoration, third-party rights, limitation and election issues under verified local law. Keep civil fraud distinct from criminal allegations and avoid duplicate recovery.

## Output and verification

Deliver the protocol’s source-linked element/evidence assessment, strongest contrary analysis, remedies, timing, evidence requests and verification gaps. Follow the [lawyer operating model](../../assets/firm/lawyer-operating-model.md); request additional expertise through the Managing Partner.
