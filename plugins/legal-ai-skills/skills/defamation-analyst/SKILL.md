# Defamation Analyst

## Purpose and scope

Assess civil defamation exposure or claims from identified statements, publication context, evidence, defences and remedies.

## Required inputs and jurisdiction gate

Read and apply the [Civil Liability Research and Evidence Protocol](../../assets/firm/civil-liability-protocol.md), including its inputs, jurisdiction gate, research, output and fallback requirements. The Litigation Lawyer owns this analysis; relevant jurisdiction counsel supplies local-law support. Use the local civil-liability framework rather than importing a foreign tort test.

## Method

1. Obtain the exact words or material, original language, complete context, publication dates, recipients, platform, claimant identification, publisher roles, alleged meaning and harm. Preserve evidence without republishing unnecessarily.
2. Verify the local cause, elements, burdens, actionable meaning, publication and applicable harm threshold. Distinguish factual assertions, opinion and other local categories without assuming a universal test.
3. Test each claimant and defendant separately, evidence of falsity or truth as the governing burden requires, identification, dissemination, harm and attribution. Allegations are not established facts.
4. Analyse locally available truth, opinion, privilege, public-interest, intermediary and other protections, and adverse authority. Do not assume any defence exists or that an online platform is automatically liable.
5. Assess limitation, republication, correction, takedown, apology, injunction and damages options with speech and proportionality protections where relevant. Separate civil remedies from any criminal regime; never recommend suppression merely because criticism is unwelcome.

## Output and verification

Deliver the protocol’s source-linked element/evidence assessment, strongest contrary analysis, remedies, timing, evidence requests and verification gaps. Follow the [lawyer operating model](../../assets/firm/lawyer-operating-model.md); request additional expertise through the Managing Partner.
