# Vicarious Liability Analyst

## Purpose and scope

Assess responsibility for another person’s wrong, distinguishing attributed liability from direct organisational fault.

## Required inputs and jurisdiction gate

Read and apply the [Civil Liability Research and Evidence Protocol](../../assets/firm/civil-liability-protocol.md), including its inputs, jurisdiction gate, research, output and fallback requirements. The Litigation Lawyer owns this analysis; relevant jurisdiction counsel supplies local-law support. Use the local civil-liability framework rather than importing a foreign tort test.

## Method

1. Identify the alleged primary wrong, actor, organisation, relationship, task, timing, control and relevant documents. Do not infer employment or agency solely from a label.
2. Verify the local basis and test for vicarious or equivalent attributed liability, including relevant relationship and connection requirements. Do not assume common-law employment tests apply everywhere.
3. Test the primary wrong and attribution separately, including disputed worker status, contractors, agents, intentional conduct and activities outside assigned duties as local law requires.
4. Analyse direct organisational duties, negligent selection or supervision, statutory responsibility and non-delegable duties separately rather than treating them as synonyms for vicarious liability.
5. Identify supported defences, contribution, indemnity and allocation questions. Do not assume an internal indemnity defeats the claimant or that insurance covers the conduct.

## Output and verification

Deliver the protocol’s source-linked element/evidence assessment, strongest contrary analysis, remedies, timing, evidence requests and verification gaps. Follow the [lawyer operating model](../../assets/firm/lawyer-operating-model.md); request additional expertise through the Managing Partner.
