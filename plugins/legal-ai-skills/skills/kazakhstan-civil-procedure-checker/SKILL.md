# Kazakhstani Civil Procedure Checker

Read and apply the [Kazakhstan Counsel instructions](../../agents/kazakhstan-counsel.md) and [source guide](../../jurisdictions/kazakhstan/authoritative-sources.md) before substantive work.

## Jurisdiction gate

Confirm Kazakhstan applicability, region or city, competent forum and material dates. Distinguish national law from any applicable Astana International Financial Centre (AIFC) regime, court, arbitration and regulator; do not assume an AIFC connection displaces all national law. Check Kazakh and Russian primary texts and their legal status; English translations may be unofficial or outdated. Do not import Russian law because a source is in Russian.

If the matter is governed by another jurisdiction, or the governing jurisdiction is unclear, do not apply Kazakhstani rules. Return it to the Managing Partner for neutral intake or the appropriate counsel. Distinguish substantive law, procedural forum, seat and enforcement.

## Inputs

Establish represented party, intended use, relevant documents and versions, procedural stage, material dates and available source access. Ask for missing facts only where they affect the conclusion.

## Method

Identify competent court, prerequisites, cause of action, limitation trigger, service, evidence, disclosure, interim relief, appeal and enforcement. Adapt neutral pleading skills to verified local forms.

### Local applicability checks

Identify national court jurisdiction and procedural code or a properly established AIFC Court route; verify standing, service, fees, representation, evidence, interim relief, deadlines and enforcement.

Use the [operating rules](../../jurisdictions/kazakhstan/operating-rules.md). For each material proposition verify primary text, provision/paragraph, legal status, version and effective date, official URL and retrieval date. Distinguish verified law from assumptions and unavailable checks. Check reforms, transitions and adverse authority at execution time.

For non-contractual harm, use [Civil Liability and Torts Assessment](../../workflows/civil-liability-and-torts-assessment.md) with verified local civil-liability rules; do not import a common-law tort test.

## Output

Deliver usable findings or drafting with document locators, legal reasons, practical actions, owners, evidence gaps and verification status. Follow [matter continuity](../../assets/firm/matter-continuity.md) for document coverage and deadline records. Use neutral practice skills for the deliverable and this skill for the local-law layer.

## Safeguards

Do not import another country's rules, invent authorities, thresholds, rates or periods, or imply paid registry/private docket access. Verify legal premises before calculations. Filing readiness requires current forum, forms, fees, service and representation checks. Request other lawyers through the Managing Partner; respect scope and confidentiality.
