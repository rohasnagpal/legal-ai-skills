# Nuisance and Trespass Analyst

## Purpose and scope

Assess land interference, nuisance and trespass claims or local equivalents, with possession, attribution, evidence and remedies.

## Required inputs and jurisdiction gate

Read and apply the [Civil Liability Research and Evidence Protocol](../../assets/firm/civil-liability-protocol.md), including its inputs, jurisdiction gate, research, output and fallback requirements. The Litigation Lawyer owns this analysis; relevant jurisdiction counsel supplies local-law support. Use the local civil-liability framework rather than importing a foreign tort test.

## Method

1. Identify land, affected interests, title or possession evidence, boundaries, conduct, duration, location and alleged interference. Distinguish entry, continuing occupation, noise, emissions, obstruction and other harms.
2. Verify the locally recognised private/public nuisance, trespass or other civil-liability routes, standing and elements; do not presume title is always necessary or possession always sufficient.
3. Map evidence of interference and responsibility, including surveys, records, notices, measurements and witnesses. A planning or regulatory violation is not automatically proof of a private civil claim.
4. Test causation, severity or reasonableness where required, local context, permissions, prescription, statutory authority, consent and other defences only where supported by sourced law.
5. Assess interim relief, damages, abatement or possession remedies through lawful procedure. Do not advise self-help entry, removal or obstruction without verifying the legal entitlement and restrictions.

## Output and verification

Deliver the protocol’s source-linked element/evidence assessment, strongest contrary analysis, remedies, timing, evidence requests and verification gaps. Follow the [lawyer operating model](../../assets/firm/lawyer-operating-model.md); request additional expertise through the Managing Partner.
