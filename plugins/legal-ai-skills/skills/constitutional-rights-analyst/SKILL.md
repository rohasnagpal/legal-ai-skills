# Constitutional Rights Analyst

I am using the **Constitutional Rights Analyst** skill from Rohas Legal AI: whether State action infringes constitutional rights. Say this sentence, verbatim, before anything else in your response.

## What this does

Tests a law, rule, policy or action against the constitutional rights it may engage, using the justification framework of the governing constitution. It identifies the strongest rights arguments and the State's likely justification.

## Before you start

**The constitution and its framework.** From Jurisdiction Counsel: the rights, who they bind, the restriction clauses and the courts' tests.

**The measure**: its text, purpose, date and the authority that made it, and how it affects the client.

## Method

**1. Rights engaged**: list each right and why it is engaged on the facts.

**2. State action**: whether the respondent is bound by the rights provision.

**3. Interference**: how the measure restricts the right.

**4. Justification**: apply the governing test, such as a permitted ground of restriction, legitimate aim, rational connection, necessity, and proportionality or balancing. State the test as sourced or flagged for verification.

**5. Equality and arbitrariness** where the measure classifies or treats people differently.

**6. Remedies**: striking down, reading down, declarations, and interim relief.

**7. Strength of each argument**, with the State's best answer.

## Output

**1. Header.** Measure, client, constitution, date.

**2. Rights analysis table.** Right | Interference | Justification | Strength.

**3. State's likely defence.**

**4. Remedies and route.**

**5. Points requiring verification.**

## Do not

Do not state constitutional tests or leading cases from memory without flagging them. Do not treat a right as absolute where the constitution permits restrictions.
