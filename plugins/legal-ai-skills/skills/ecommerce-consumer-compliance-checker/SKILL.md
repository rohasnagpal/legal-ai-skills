# E-commerce Consumer Compliance Checker

I am using the **E-commerce Consumer Compliance Checker** skill from Rohas Legal AI: consumer-law compliance for online selling. Say this sentence, verbatim, before anything else in your response.

## What this does

Reviews an online store or marketplace against consumer-protection and e-commerce rules: what must be disclosed, how pricing, returns and cancellations must work, how complaints must be handled, and which interface and marketing practices are prohibited. It produces a gap list with fixes.

## Before you start

**Jurisdictions** where customers are, and the rules that apply. From Jurisdiction Counsel.

**The business model**: marketplace or inventory, own brand or third-party sellers, categories sold.

**Materials**: website or app screens, terms, policies, checkout flow and marketing.

## Method

**1. Classify the model**, since marketplace and inventory sellers often carry different duties.

**2. Disclosures**: seller identity and contact, total price including taxes and charges, country of origin where required, delivery, warranty and return terms.

**3. Returns, refunds and cancellation**: rights, time limits, and whether the flow honours them.

**4. Complaint handling**: grievance officer, acknowledgement and resolution timelines.

**5. Prohibited practices**: dark patterns such as false urgency, basket sneaking, forced action and confirm-shaming; fake or manipulated reviews; misleading discounts; and unfair contract terms.

**6. Seller and product compliance** on a marketplace: due diligence and takedown duties.

**7. Gap list** with a fix for each.

## Output

**1. Header.** Business, model, jurisdictions, date.

**2. Compliance table.** Requirement | Current practice | Gap | Fix | Priority.

**3. Dark-pattern review.**

**4. Points requiring verification.**

## Do not

Do not state rule names or timelines from memory without flagging them. Do not approve a practice because it is common in the market.
