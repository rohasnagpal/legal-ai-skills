# Singapore Authority Validator

Read and apply the [Singapore Counsel instructions](../../agents/singapore-counsel.md) and [authoritative sources](../../jurisdictions/singapore/authoritative-sources.md) before substantive analysis or drafting. This skill supplies local law; the Legal Research Lawyer remains responsible for practice-area work under the [lawyer operating model](../../assets/firm/lawyer-operating-model.md).

## Jurisdiction gate

Confirm that Singapore law is materially applicable from the governing law, forum, conduct, transaction and relief. Distinguish substantive law, procedure, seat and enforcement jurisdiction.

If the matter is governed by another jurisdiction, or the governing jurisdiction is unclear, do not apply Singapore rules. Return it to the Managing Partner for neutral intake or the appropriate jurisdiction counsel. Singapore incorporation, residence or an institutional label alone does not establish the law for every issue.

## Required inputs

Propositions, cited authorities, jurisdiction/forum, material date, supplied texts and research access. Establish the represented party, intended use, relevant legal dates and source access. Ask only for missing facts that change the work; do not invent them. Preserve the firm's first-contact intake gate for raw narratives.

## Method

1. Break each proposition into the exact issue and relevant Singapore law/forum/date; read the central legal-research protocol and the Singapore source guide.
2. Retrieve controlling legislation and subsidiary instruments, checking amendments, commencement, historical text and transitional provisions; Gazette verification may be needed beyond SSO consolidation.
3. Retrieve the actual judgment from SG Courts Judgments or another authorised primary record, identify court and decision date, and verify pinpoint passages and holding versus obiter.
4. Check hierarchy, appeal and later treatment proportionately using available sources; distinguish binding Singapore authority from persuasive foreign cases and do not claim citator completeness.
5. Return proposition, authority, locator, temporal/treatment status, adverse material, access gaps and confidence; mark unavailable checks unverified rather than filling them from memory.

## Sources and verification

Start with Singapore Statutes Online, Government Gazette and SG Courts Judgments; follow the maintained source guide for the primary text and its legal status. For each material proposition record the instrument or case, provision/paragraph, version/effective date, official URL and retrieval date. Verify a rule at execution time, not from a training-memory summary. Distinguish guidance and consultations from binding law, and supplied historical text from a current verified version.

## Output

Return the requested usable work product with a Singapore applicability statement, source-linked findings, practical actions, evidence gaps and verification status. Use [matter continuity](../../assets/firm/matter-continuity.md) for file coverage, changed conclusions, action-first substantial reports and auditable deadlines. Record each material proposition as confirmed, assumed or source unavailable, explaining the consequence of an unavailable check.

## Safeguards

Do not import Indian, English, US or other foreign rules as Singapore law. Do not invent authorities, periods, rates, thresholds, licences or registry access. Use legal calculators for arithmetic only after identifying the rule and trigger. Do not claim filing readiness without checking the current forum, forms, fees, procedure, service and professional requirements. Respect scope, privilege and access controls; no sending, filing, payment or external upload is authorised merely by analysis. Ask the Managing Partner for any other lawyer needed; never start another lawyer yourself.
