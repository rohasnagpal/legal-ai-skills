# UAE Public Law Remedies Checker

Read and apply the [UAE Counsel instructions](../../agents/uae-counsel.md) and [source guide](../../jurisdictions/uae/authoritative-sources.md) before substantive work.

## Jurisdiction gate

Identify the emirate, mainland or particular free zone, federal/local forum, DIFC or ADGM nexus and governing language. Do not treat all free zones as DIFC or ADGM. Determine the authoritative Arabic text and status of any English translation.

If the matter is governed by another jurisdiction, or the governing jurisdiction is unclear, do not apply UAE rules. Return it to the Managing Partner for neutral intake or the appropriate counsel. Distinguish substantive law, procedural forum, seat and enforcement.

## Inputs

Establish represented party, intended use, relevant documents and versions, procedural stage, material dates and available source access. Ask for missing facts only where they affect the conclusion.

## Method

Identify decision, legal power, reasons, standing and relief. Distinguish statutory appeal, merits review, judicial review and constitutional challenge; verify exhaustion, urgency and time limits.

### Local applicability checks

Identify federal/emirate public body, administrative challenge route, standing, immunity and required notices; distinguish DIFC/ADGM supervisory jurisdiction.

Use the [operating rules](../../jurisdictions/uae/operating-rules.md). For each material proposition verify primary text, provision/paragraph, legal status, version and effective date, official URL and retrieval date. Distinguish verified law from assumptions and unavailable checks. Check reforms, transitions and adverse authority at execution time.

## Output

Deliver usable findings or drafting with document locators, legal reasons, practical actions, owners, evidence gaps and verification status. Follow [matter continuity](../../assets/firm/matter-continuity.md) for document coverage and deadline records. Use neutral practice skills for the deliverable and this skill for the local-law layer.

## Safeguards

Do not import another country's rules, invent authorities, thresholds, rates or periods, or imply paid registry/private docket access. Verify legal premises before calculations. Filing readiness requires current forum, forms, fees, service and representation checks. Request other lawyers through the Managing Partner; respect scope and confidentiality.
