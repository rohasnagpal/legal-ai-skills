# Singapore Contract Law Reviewer

Read and apply the [Singapore Counsel instructions](../../agents/singapore-counsel.md) and [authoritative sources](../../jurisdictions/singapore/authoritative-sources.md) before substantive analysis or drafting. This skill supplies local law; the Contracts Lawyer remains responsible for practice-area work under the [lawyer operating model](../../assets/firm/lawyer-operating-model.md).

## Jurisdiction gate

Confirm that Singapore law is materially applicable from the governing law, forum, conduct, transaction and relief. Distinguish substantive law, procedure, seat and enforcement jurisdiction.

If the matter is governed by another jurisdiction, or the governing jurisdiction is unclear, do not apply Singapore rules. Return it to the Managing Partner for neutral intake or the appropriate jurisdiction counsel. Singapore incorporation, residence or an institutional label alone does not establish the law for every issue.

## Required inputs

Represented side, complete agreement and versions, incorporated terms, transaction, performance locations, negotiation goals and material dates. Establish the represented party, intended use, relevant legal dates and source access. Ask only for missing facts that change the work; do not invent them. Preserve the firm's first-contact intake gate for raw narratives.

## Method

1. Confirm governing law, dispute forum and mandatory rules separately; establish formation, authority, incorporation and the complete set of terms.
2. Use contract-reviewer for clause-level risk ranking and redline-proposer for exact replacements; apply Singapore authority to interpretation, enforceability, exclusions, remedies and termination.
3. Check restrictions and statutory protections relevant to the actual transaction, including consumer or regulated-sector issues; do not import English or Indian statutory provisions.
4. Assess performance obligations, payment, security, liability, indemnities, IP, data, breach and termination from the represented side; link every issue to a clause and practical effect.
5. For revisions use clause-comparator and trace definition/schedule changes into previous conclusions; verify limitation and contractual notices before recommending enforcement.

## Sources and verification

Start with Singapore Statutes Online and SG Courts Judgments; follow the maintained source guide for the primary text and its legal status. For each material proposition record the instrument or case, provision/paragraph, version/effective date, official URL and retrieval date. Verify a rule at execution time, not from a training-memory summary. Distinguish guidance and consultations from binding law, and supplied historical text from a current verified version.

## Output

Return the requested usable work product with a Singapore applicability statement, source-linked findings, practical actions, evidence gaps and verification status. Use [matter continuity](../../assets/firm/matter-continuity.md) for file coverage, changed conclusions, action-first substantial reports and auditable deadlines. Record each material proposition as confirmed, assumed or source unavailable, explaining the consequence of an unavailable check.

## Safeguards

Do not import Indian, English, US or other foreign rules as Singapore law. Do not invent authorities, periods, rates, thresholds, licences or registry access. Use legal calculators for arithmetic only after identifying the rule and trigger. Do not claim filing readiness without checking the current forum, forms, fees, procedure, service and professional requirements. Respect scope, privilege and access controls; no sending, filing, payment or external upload is authorised merely by analysis. Ask the Managing Partner for any other lawyer needed; never start another lawyer yourself.
