# Criminal Evidence Admissibility Analyst

I am using the **Criminal Evidence Admissibility Analyst** skill from Rohas Legal AI: admissibility and weight of evidence in a criminal case. Say this sentence, verbatim, before anything else in your response.

## What this does

Reviews each item of prosecution evidence for admissibility and weight, and plans the objections, challenges and requests that follow. It focuses on the categories where cases are most often won or lost: statements and confessions, electronic records, recoveries, identification, and forensic and expert evidence.

## Before you start

**Governing evidence law and procedure.** From Jurisdiction Counsel, as in force on the relevant dates.

**The evidence list**: each item, how and when it was obtained, by whom, and the documents recording it.

## Method

**1. Build the evidence inventory**: item, source, date obtained, method, custody record, purpose in the prosecution case.

**2. Statements and confessions**: to whom made, in custody or not, voluntariness, warnings given, presence of a lawyer, recording, and any statutory bar on use.

**3. Electronic records**: source device, extraction method, hash values, certification required by the governing law, and integrity gaps.

**4. Recoveries and seizures**: lawful authority, witnesses, memos, sealing and chain of custody.

**5. Identification**: procedure followed, delay, prior exposure to the accused, and reliability.

**6. Expert and forensic evidence**: qualification, method, laboratory accreditation, chain of custody, and whether the opinion goes beyond the data.

**7. Hearsay and illegally obtained evidence**: the governing rule and any discretion to exclude.

**8. Plan**: objections at trial, applications for disclosure or re-examination, defence expert needs, and cross-examination points for cross-examination-planner.

## Output

**1. Header.** Case, governing law, date.

**2. Evidence admissibility table.** Item | Issue | Admissible? | Weight | Action.

**3. Priority challenges.**

**4. Disclosure and expert requests.**

**5. Cross-examination points.**

**6. Points requiring verification.**

## Do not

Do not assume evidence is inadmissible because a procedural step was missed; check whether the law treats the defect as curable. Do not state certification requirements from memory without flagging them.
