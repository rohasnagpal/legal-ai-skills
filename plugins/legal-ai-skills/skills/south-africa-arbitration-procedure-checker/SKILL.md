# South African Arbitration Procedure Checker

Read and apply the [South Africa Counsel instructions](../../agents/south-africa-counsel.md) and [source guide](../../jurisdictions/south-africa/authoritative-sources.md) before substantive work.

## Jurisdiction gate

Identify national, provincial and municipal rules, competent court/division and material dates. Consider constitutional rights, customary law and personal-law applicability on evidence; do not assume either from name or ethnicity.

If the matter is governed by another jurisdiction, or the governing jurisdiction is unclear, do not apply South African rules. Return it to the Managing Partner for neutral intake or the appropriate counsel. Distinguish substantive law, procedural forum, seat and enforcement.

## Inputs

Establish represented party, intended use, relevant documents and versions, procedural stage, material dates and available source access. Ask for missing facts only where they affect the conclusion.

## Method

Separate governing law, seat, institution and enforcement. Verify arbitrability, appointment, interim relief, objections, challenge and recognition; use the existing arbitration workflow.

### Local applicability checks

Separate domestic and international arbitration frameworks, seat, public-body constraints and award enforcement.

Use the [operating rules](../../jurisdictions/south-africa/operating-rules.md). For each material proposition verify primary text, provision/paragraph, legal status, version and effective date, official URL and retrieval date. Distinguish verified law from assumptions and unavailable checks. Check reforms, transitions and adverse authority at execution time.

## Output

Deliver usable findings or drafting with document locators, legal reasons, practical actions, owners, evidence gaps and verification status. Follow [matter continuity](../../assets/firm/matter-continuity.md) for document coverage and deadline records. Use neutral practice skills for the deliverable and this skill for the local-law layer.

## Safeguards

Do not import another country's rules, invent authorities, thresholds, rates or periods, or imply paid registry/private docket access. Verify legal premises before calculations. Filing readiness requires current forum, forms, fees, service and representation checks. Request other lawyers through the Managing Partner; respect scope and confidentiality.
