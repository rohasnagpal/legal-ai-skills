# Brazilian Employment Law Applicability Checker

Read and apply the [Brazil Counsel instructions](../../agents/brazil-counsel.md) and [source guide](../../jurisdictions/brazil/authoritative-sources.md) before substantive work.

## Jurisdiction gate

Confirm Brazil applicability, state, municipality, competent forum and material dates. Distinguish federal, state and municipal competence and specialist justice branches. Verify authoritative Portuguese text, amendments and translation status; do not import Portuguese or other Lusophone law.

If the matter is governed by another jurisdiction, or the governing jurisdiction is unclear, do not apply Brazilian rules. Return it to the Managing Partner for neutral intake or the appropriate counsel. Distinguish substantive law, procedural forum, seat and enforcement.

## Inputs

Establish represented party, intended use, relevant documents and versions, procedural stage, material dates and available source access. Ask for missing facts only where they affect the conclusion.

## Method

Identify status, workplace, sector, collective instruments and protected characteristics. Verify minimum terms, termination, remedies and competent dispute gateway with trigger-based deadlines.

### Local applicability checks

Check CLT coverage, worker status, collective agreements, termination, employment rights and labour-court competence.

Use the [operating rules](../../jurisdictions/brazil/operating-rules.md). For each material proposition verify primary text, provision/paragraph, legal status, version and effective date, official URL and retrieval date. Distinguish verified law from assumptions and unavailable checks. Check reforms, transitions and adverse authority at execution time.

## Output

Deliver usable findings or drafting with document locators, legal reasons, practical actions, owners, evidence gaps and verification status. Follow [matter continuity](../../assets/firm/matter-continuity.md) for document coverage and deadline records. Use neutral practice skills for the deliverable and this skill for the local-law layer.

## Safeguards

Do not import another country's rules, invent authorities, thresholds, rates or periods, or imply paid registry/private docket access. Verify legal premises before calculations. Filing readiness requires current forum, forms, fees, service and representation checks. Request other lawyers through the Managing Partner; respect scope and confidentiality.
