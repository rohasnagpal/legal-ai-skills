# Irish Investigation and Evidence Checker

Read and apply the [Ireland Counsel instructions](../../agents/ireland-counsel.md) and [source guide](../../jurisdictions/ireland/authoritative-sources.md) before substantive work.

## Jurisdiction gate

Confirm Republic of Ireland applicability; Northern Ireland belongs to the UK route. Identify Irish and applicable EU law, transposition, direct applicability and CJEU authority. Do not treat post-independence English law or UK reforms as automatically Irish law.

If the matter is governed by another jurisdiction, or the governing jurisdiction is unclear, do not apply Irish rules. Return it to the Managing Partner for neutral intake or the appropriate counsel. Distinguish substantive law, procedural forum, seat and enforcement.

## Inputs

Establish represented party, intended use, relevant documents and versions, procedural stage, material dates and available source access. Ask for missing facts only where they affect the conclusion.

## Method

Define mandate, lawful collection and privilege. Preserve provenance, custody, interviews and competing explanations; verify privacy, surveillance, disclosure and regulator-reporting restrictions.

### Local applicability checks

Assess Irish/EU data protection, workplace procedure, privilege and protected-disclosure rules; distinguish internal findings from Garda or regulator powers.

Use the [operating rules](../../jurisdictions/ireland/operating-rules.md). For each material proposition verify primary text, provision/paragraph, legal status, version and effective date, official URL and retrieval date. Distinguish verified law from assumptions and unavailable checks. Check reforms, transitions and adverse authority at execution time.

## Output

Deliver usable findings or drafting with document locators, legal reasons, practical actions, owners, evidence gaps and verification status. Follow [matter continuity](../../assets/firm/matter-continuity.md) for document coverage and deadline records. Use neutral practice skills for the deliverable and this skill for the local-law layer.

## Safeguards

Do not import another country's rules, invent authorities, thresholds, rates or periods, or imply paid registry/private docket access. Verify legal premises before calculations. Filing readiness requires current forum, forms, fees, service and representation checks. Request other lawyers through the Managing Partner; respect scope and confidentiality.
