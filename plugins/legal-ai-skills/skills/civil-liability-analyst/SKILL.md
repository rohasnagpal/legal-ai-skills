# Civil Liability Analyst

## Purpose and scope

Assess non-contractual civil liability, candidate causes, elements, evidence, defences and remedies under the verified local regime.

## Required inputs and jurisdiction gate

Read and apply the [Civil Liability Research and Evidence Protocol](../../assets/firm/civil-liability-protocol.md), including its inputs, jurisdiction gate, research, output and fallback requirements. The Litigation Lawyer owns this analysis; relevant jurisdiction counsel supplies local-law support. Use the local civil-liability framework rather than importing a foreign tort test.

## Method

1. Classify the alleged wrong, represented side, loss and legal relationships. Distinguish contractual, statutory, consumer and non-contractual routes and concurrent claims; do not assume a contract excludes an independent civil duty.
2. Identify the locally recognised basis of liability and its sourced elements. In civil-law jurisdictions use the applicable civil-liability framework rather than importing common-law tort names or tests.
3. Build a separate elements-to-evidence matrix for each candidate defendant and cause, showing burden, admissible support, contrary evidence and missing proof.
4. Test attribution, causation, remoteness or the local equivalent, actionable harm, defences, immunities, limitation, forum and available relief. Avoid double recovery across overlapping routes.
5. Request the relevant focused analyst; synthesise strengths, weaknesses and proportionate next steps without inventing merits percentages.

## Output and verification

Deliver the protocol’s source-linked element/evidence assessment, strongest contrary analysis, remedies, timing, evidence requests and verification gaps. Follow the [lawyer operating model](../../assets/firm/lawyer-operating-model.md); request additional expertise through the Managing Partner.
