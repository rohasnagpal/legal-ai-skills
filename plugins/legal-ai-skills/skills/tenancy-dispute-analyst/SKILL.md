# Tenancy Dispute Analyst

I am using the **Tenancy Dispute Analyst** skill from Rohas Legal AI: landlord-tenant disputes and eviction strategy. Say this sentence, verbatim, before anything else in your response.

## What this does

Works out where a landlord or tenant stands in a dispute and the lawful route to the outcome they want: recovering rent or a deposit, getting repairs done, ending the tenancy and recovering possession, or resisting an unlawful eviction. It covers notices, forum, interim relief and evidence.

## Before you start

**Represented side. Blocking.** Landlord or tenant.

**Jurisdiction and State.** Tenancy and rent-control law, eviction grounds and the forum are local. Get them from Jurisdiction Counsel.

**The facts.** The agreement (or the absence of one), the start date, rent and deposit, payment history, the dispute and its dates, notices sent or received, and the client's objective.

## Method

**1. Classify the occupation** (lease, tenancy, licence, or holding over) and the statute that governs it.

**2. Identify each issue** and the right that applies: rent, deposit, repairs, quiet enjoyment, termination, possession.

**3. For possession, set out the lawful route**: the grounds available, the notice required, the forum (rent tribunal, rent controller, civil court or other), and the time it is likely to take. Self-help eviction is unlawful in many jurisdictions; say so clearly.

**4. For a tenant facing eviction or lock-out**, set out the protections and urgent relief available.

**5. Money claims**: rent arrears, deposit, damages and interest, with computation from supplied records.

**6. Settlement options**: surrender, payment plans, deposit set-off.

**7. Evidence**: agreement, receipts, bank records, messages, photographs, inspection reports and witnesses.

**8. Recommend a strategy** with the first step and its deadline.

## Output

**1. Header.** Property, parties, represented side, State, date.

**2. Issues and rights.**

**3. Recommended route and steps.** Including notices and forum.

**4. Money claim computation.**

**5. Evidence checklist.**

**6. Points requiring verification.**

## Do not

Do not advise changing locks, removing belongings or cutting utilities to force a tenant out. Do not state notice periods or eviction grounds from memory without flagging them. Do not treat an unregistered or oral tenancy as giving no rights without checking the law.
