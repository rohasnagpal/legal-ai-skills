# New Zealand Banking and Finance Regulatory Checker

Read and apply the [New Zealand Counsel instructions](../../agents/new-zealand-counsel.md) and [source guide](../../jurisdictions/new-zealand/authoritative-sources.md) before substantive work.

## Jurisdiction gate

Confirm New Zealand applicability, forum and material dates. Treat Te Tiriti o Waitangi/Treaty of Waitangi, tikanga Māori and Māori land issues as distinct questions where relevant; do not import Australian law or assume Cook Islands, Niue or Tokelau share the same regime.

If the matter is governed by another jurisdiction, or the governing jurisdiction is unclear, do not apply New Zealand rules. Return it to the Managing Partner for neutral intake or the appropriate counsel. Distinguish substantive law, procedural forum, seat and enforcement.

## Inputs

Establish represented party, intended use, relevant documents and versions, procedural stage, material dates and available source access. Ask for missing facts only where they affect the conclusion.

## Method

Identify lender, borrower, activity and regulator; verify licensing, lending/consumer credit rules, guarantees, security creation/perfection, priority, enforcement and insolvency interaction.

### Local applicability checks

Identify Reserve Bank, FMA and consumer-credit supervision, lender obligations and PPSR security perfection; verify commencement of regulatory changes.

Use the [operating rules](../../jurisdictions/new-zealand/operating-rules.md). For each material proposition verify primary text, provision/paragraph, legal status, version and effective date, official URL and retrieval date. Distinguish verified law from assumptions and unavailable checks. Check reforms, transitions and adverse authority at execution time.

## Output

Deliver usable findings or drafting with document locators, legal reasons, practical actions, owners, evidence gaps and verification status. Follow [matter continuity](../../assets/firm/matter-continuity.md) for document coverage and deadline records. Use neutral practice skills for the deliverable and this skill for the local-law layer.

## Safeguards

Do not import another country's rules, invent authorities, thresholds, rates or periods, or imply paid registry/private docket access. Verify legal premises before calculations. Filing readiness requires current forum, forms, fees, service and representation checks. Request other lawyers through the Managing Partner; respect scope and confidentiality.
