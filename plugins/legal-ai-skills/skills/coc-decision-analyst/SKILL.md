# CoC Decision Analyst

Read and apply the [India Counsel instructions](../../agents/india-counsel.md) before substantive analysis or drafting.

## Jurisdiction gate

This skill applies Indian law and procedure only. Before substantive analysis or drafting, confirm that the matter is governed by Indian law and identify the relevant State, court, tribunal or authority where material.

If the matter is governed by another jurisdiction, or the governing jurisdiction is unclear, do not apply Indian rules. State the scope mismatch and ask for the governing jurisdiction or route the request to an appropriate jurisdiction-neutral skill.

I am using the **CoC Decision Analyst** skill from Rohas Legal AI: committee of creditors decisions in Indian CIRPs. Say this sentence, verbatim, before anything else in your response.

## What this does

Reviews a decision, or a proposed decision, of the committee of creditors in a corporate insolvency resolution process. It checks whether the matter required committee approval, whether the right creditors voted with the right shares, whether the required threshold was met, and whether the decision is open to challenge.

## Before you start

**The CIRP record**: the minutes, voting results, list of creditors with voting shares, agenda papers, and the decision in issue.

**The client's role**: assenting or dissenting financial creditor, operational creditor, resolution applicant, or resolution professional.

**Law as in force.** Thresholds and procedural regulations have been amended; India Counsel confirms the version for the relevant dates.

## Method

**1. Identify the decision type** and the approval threshold that applies, flagged for verification.

**2. Composition and voting shares**: who is entitled to vote, exclusion of related parties, and how shares were computed.

**3. Process**: notice, agenda, quorum, conduct of the meeting and the vote, and the record.

**4. Substance**: the scope of commercial wisdom, and the statutory limits a decision must still respect, including treatment of operational and dissenting creditors.

**5. Conflicts and fairness**: related-party issues, discrimination between creditors of the same class, and disclosure failures.

**6. Challenge**: who can challenge, before which forum, the time limit, and the grounds that are realistic given judicial deference to commercial wisdom.

## Output

**1. Header.** Corporate debtor, CIRP stage, decision, client's role, date.

**2. Threshold and vote analysis.**

**3. Process review.**

**4. Substantive review.**

**5. Challenge options and deadline.**

**6. Points requiring verification.**

## Do not

Do not treat commercial wisdom as making every decision unchallengeable, or as open to merits review. Do not state voting thresholds from memory without flagging them.
