# Australian Insolvency Applicability Checker

Read and apply the [Australia Counsel instructions](../../agents/australia-counsel.md) and [authoritative sources](../../jurisdictions/australia/authoritative-sources.md) before substantive analysis or drafting. This skill supplies local law; the Insolvency Lawyer remains responsible for practice-area work under the [lawyer operating model](../../assets/firm/lawyer-operating-model.md).

## Jurisdiction gate

Confirm that Australian law is materially applicable from the governing law, forum, conduct, transaction and relief. Identify the relevant state or territory and Commonwealth law for each issue. Distinguish substantive law, procedure, seat and enforcement jurisdiction.

If the matter is governed by another jurisdiction, or the governing jurisdiction is unclear, do not apply Australian rules. Return it to the Managing Partner for neutral intake or the appropriate jurisdiction counsel. Australian incorporation, residence or an institutional label alone does not establish the law for every issue.

## Required inputs

Entity or individual, debts, security, solvency evidence, creditor demands, appointments, forum and dates. Establish the represented party, intended use, material dates and source access. Preserve the first-contact intake gate and ask only for facts that affect the work.

## Method

1. Separate corporate insolvency from personal bankruptcy and identify the applicable legislation, forum and regulator.
2. Assess restructuring, administration, liquidation or bankruptcy routes against eligibility and evidence, verifying current thresholds and notices.
3. Check secured-creditor rights, priorities, stays, director exposure, voidable transactions and cross-border recognition where material.
4. Use creditor claim and restructuring skills to build options, evidence and verified deadlines, without importing Indian CIRP procedure.

## Sources and verification

Start with the official sources for this practice area in the maintained source guide and verify the primary text and its legal status. For each material proposition record the instrument or case, provision/paragraph, version/effective date, official URL and retrieval date. Verify a rule at execution time, not from a training-memory summary. Distinguish guidance and consultations from binding law, and supplied historical text from a current verified version.

## Output

Return the requested usable work product with a Australian applicability statement, source-linked findings, practical actions, evidence gaps and verification status. Use [matter continuity](../../assets/firm/matter-continuity.md) for file coverage, changed conclusions, action-first substantial reports and auditable deadlines. Record each material proposition as confirmed, assumed or source unavailable, explaining the consequence of an unavailable check.

## Safeguards

Do not import Indian, English, US or other foreign rules as Australian law. Do not invent authorities, periods, rates, thresholds, licences or registry access. Use legal calculators for arithmetic only after identifying the rule and trigger. Do not claim filing readiness without checking the current forum, forms, fees, procedure, service and professional requirements. Respect scope, privilege and access controls; no sending, filing, payment or external upload is authorised merely by analysis. Ask the Managing Partner for any other lawyer needed; never start another lawyer yourself.
