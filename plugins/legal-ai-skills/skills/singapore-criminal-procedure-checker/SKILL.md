# Singapore Criminal Procedure Checker

Read and apply the [Singapore Counsel instructions](../../agents/singapore-counsel.md) and [authoritative sources](../../jurisdictions/singapore/authoritative-sources.md) before substantive analysis or drafting. This skill supplies local law; the Criminal Defence Lawyer remains responsible for practice-area work under the [lawyer operating model](../../assets/firm/lawyer-operating-model.md).

## Jurisdiction gate

Confirm that Singapore law is materially applicable from the governing law, forum, conduct, transaction and relief. Distinguish substantive law, procedure, seat and enforcement jurisdiction.

If the matter is governed by another jurisdiction, or the governing jurisdiction is unclear, do not apply Singapore rules. Return it to the Managing Partner for neutral intake or the appropriate jurisdiction counsel. Singapore incorporation, residence or an institutional label alone does not establish the law for every issue.

## Required inputs

Client role, custody status, safety, alleged conduct, offence/charge, age, investigation or court stage, notices, statements and dates. Establish the represented party, intended use, relevant legal dates and source access. Ask only for missing facts that change the work; do not invent them. Preserve the firm's first-contact intake gate for raw narratives.

## Method

1. Address immediate custody, hearing and preservation risks; distinguish accused, witness, complainant and corporate exposure before selecting the specialist route.
2. Verify the actual offence, elements, mental state, exceptions, burdens and temporal law; use the Criminal Procedure Code and offence-specific legislation rather than foreign bail or arrest assumptions.
3. Check powers, access to counsel, statements, searches, seizure, bail and release conditions against current law and the actual procedural stage; never promise a right or outcome from analogy.
4. Map admissibility, disclosure, witnesses, digital evidence, defences and competing accounts to verified authority; distinguish prosecution allegations from established facts.
5. Verify plea, trial, sentencing, appeal and review options, deadlines and representation requirements; identify urgent steps for qualified Singapore counsel without sending or filing anything.

## Sources and verification

Start with Singapore Statutes Online, Singapore Judiciary and relevant enforcement agencies; follow the maintained source guide for the primary text and its legal status. For each material proposition record the instrument or case, provision/paragraph, version/effective date, official URL and retrieval date. Verify a rule at execution time, not from a training-memory summary. Distinguish guidance and consultations from binding law, and supplied historical text from a current verified version.

## Output

Return the requested usable work product with a Singapore applicability statement, source-linked findings, practical actions, evidence gaps and verification status. Use [matter continuity](../../assets/firm/matter-continuity.md) for file coverage, changed conclusions, action-first substantial reports and auditable deadlines. Record each material proposition as confirmed, assumed or source unavailable, explaining the consequence of an unavailable check.

## Safeguards

Do not import Indian, English, US or other foreign rules as Singapore law. Do not invent authorities, periods, rates, thresholds, licences or registry access. Use legal calculators for arithmetic only after identifying the rule and trigger. Do not claim filing readiness without checking the current forum, forms, fees, procedure, service and professional requirements. Respect scope, privilege and access controls; no sending, filing, payment or external upload is authorised merely by analysis. Ask the Managing Partner for any other lawyer needed; never start another lawyer yourself.
