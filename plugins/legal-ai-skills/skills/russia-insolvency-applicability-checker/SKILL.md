# Russian Insolvency Applicability Checker

Read and apply the [Russia Counsel instructions](../../agents/russia-counsel.md) and [source guide](../../jurisdictions/russia/authoritative-sources.md) before substantive work.

## Jurisdiction gate

Confirm the Russian Federation nexus, federal subject, competent forum and material dates. Separate Russian domestic law from foreign sanctions, export controls and countermeasures; request other counsel through the Managing Partner. Verify authoritative Russian text and translation status; identify disputed-territory applicability without assuming recognition.

If the matter is governed by another jurisdiction, or the governing jurisdiction is unclear, do not apply Russian rules. Return it to the Managing Partner for neutral intake or the appropriate counsel. Distinguish substantive law, procedural forum, seat and enforcement.

## Inputs

Establish represented party, intended use, relevant documents and versions, procedural stage, material dates and available source access. Ask for missing facts only where they affect the conclusion.

## Method

Identify debtor type, solvency evidence and corporate/personal regime; verify commencement thresholds, moratoria, claim admission, priorities, restructuring, avoidance and cross-border recognition.

### Local applicability checks

Check corporate versus individual insolvency, debtor eligibility, moratoria, priorities, avoidance and cross-border recognition.

Use the [operating rules](../../jurisdictions/russia/operating-rules.md). For each material proposition verify primary text, provision/paragraph, legal status, version and effective date, official URL and retrieval date. Distinguish verified law from assumptions and unavailable checks. Check reforms, transitions and adverse authority at execution time.

## Output

Deliver usable findings or drafting with document locators, legal reasons, practical actions, owners, evidence gaps and verification status. Follow [matter continuity](../../assets/firm/matter-continuity.md) for document coverage and deadline records. Use neutral practice skills for the deliverable and this skill for the local-law layer.

## Safeguards

Do not import another country's rules, invent authorities, thresholds, rates or periods, or imply paid registry/private docket access. Verify legal premises before calculations. Filing readiness requires current forum, forms, fees, service and representation checks. Request other lawyers through the Managing Partner; respect scope and confidentiality.
