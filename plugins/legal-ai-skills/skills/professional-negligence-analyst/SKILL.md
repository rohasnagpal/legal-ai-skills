# Professional Negligence Analyst

## Purpose and scope

Assess civil claims against professional advisers and practitioners, including engagement scope, standard, causation and expert evidence.

## Required inputs and jurisdiction gate

Read and apply the [Civil Liability Research and Evidence Protocol](../../assets/firm/civil-liability-protocol.md), including its inputs, jurisdiction gate, research, output and fallback requirements. The Litigation Lawyer owns this analysis; relevant jurisdiction counsel supplies local-law support. Use the local civil-liability framework rather than importing a foreign tort test.

## Method

1. Identify the profession, client and any third-party claimant, engagement scope, retainer or consent records, professional acts, dates and alleged loss.
2. Verify concurrent contract, civil-liability, statutory and disciplinary routes separately. A regulatory or ethical breach is not automatically a successful damages claim.
3. Identify the locally applicable professional standard, scope of responsibility, disclosure or informed-consent duties where relevant, and expert evidence requirements. Do not decide medical or technical standards from legal reasoning alone.
4. Compare the advice or treatment with contemporaneous instructions, records and reasonable alternatives; distinguish hindsight from evidence of a deficient professional act.
5. Test reliance and counterfactual outcome, causation and recoverable loss, including any locally recognised lost-chance theory only if sourced. Address exclusions, limitation and notification issues on evidence; do not assume insurance coverage.

## Output and verification

Deliver the protocol’s source-linked element/evidence assessment, strongest contrary analysis, remedies, timing, evidence requests and verification gaps. Follow the [lawyer operating model](../../assets/firm/lawyer-operating-model.md); request additional expertise through the Managing Partner.
