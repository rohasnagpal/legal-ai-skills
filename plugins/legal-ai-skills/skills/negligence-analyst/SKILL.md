# Negligence Analyst

## Purpose and scope

Assess alleged negligent conduct, duty, standard, breach, causation, damage and defences under verified local law.

## Required inputs and jurisdiction gate

Read and apply the [Civil Liability Research and Evidence Protocol](../../assets/firm/civil-liability-protocol.md), including its inputs, jurisdiction gate, research, output and fallback requirements. The Litigation Lawyer owns this analysis; relevant jurisdiction counsel supplies local-law support. Use the local civil-liability framework rather than importing a foreign tort test.

## Method

1. Identify the act or omission, claimant-defendant relationship, alleged harm and legally relevant dates; identify personal injury, property, economic and other loss separately.
2. Verify the local duty or equivalent basis, applicable standard and treatment of omissions, third-party acts and pure economic loss where material. Do not presume every foreseeable loss creates a duty.
3. Compare conduct with the sourced standard using contemporaneous evidence; distinguish an adverse outcome from proof of breach and identify expert evidence needed.
4. Test factual and legal causation separately, alternative causes, intervening acts, scope of responsibility, loss and mitigation. Do not assume temporal sequence proves causation.
5. Analyse supported defences, contributory fault, consent, statutory protections, immunities and time bars only under verified local rules.

## Output and verification

Deliver the protocol’s source-linked element/evidence assessment, strongest contrary analysis, remedies, timing, evidence requests and verification gaps. Follow the [lawyer operating model](../../assets/firm/lawyer-operating-model.md); request additional expertise through the Managing Partner.
