# Civil Liability and Torts Assessment

## Trigger and objective

Use for claimant or defendant assessment of alleged civil wrongs, negligence, professional negligence, defamation, nuisance, trespass, deceit or responsibility for another person's wrong. Produce a sourced liability, evidence and remedy assessment under the applicable local civil-liability framework; do not assume common-law tort categories apply universally.

## Required inputs

Represented party, jurisdiction and relevant local unit, forum, relevant dates, actors and relationships, precise conduct or words, alleged harm, available contemporaneous records, notices, proceedings, urgency and desired outcome. Distinguish evidence from narrative and preserve the first-contact gate. Ask material missing questions before substantive conclusions.

## Branches

- **Claimant**, **defendant** or **pre-dispute exposure**.
- **General civil liability**, **negligence**, **professional negligence**, **defamation**, **land interference**, **misrepresentation** or **attributed liability**, including concurrent routes where supported.
- **Urgent preservation or interim relief**, **merits assessment** or **litigation preparation**.

The Matter Owner records the branches. Statutory, contractual, consumer and non-contractual claims may overlap; verify each without double recovery.

## Matter Owner and team

The [Litigation Lawyer](../agents/litigation-lawyer.md) leads with applicable jurisdiction counsel. Only the Managing Partner starts supporting lawyers. Request Consumer Protection for product and consumer routes, Real Estate for land interests, Employment for employment-related issues, Contracts for retainers and disclaimers, Investigations for evidence integrity and Legal Research for authority checks as needed.

Start with [civil-liability-analyst](../skills/civil-liability-analyst/SKILL.md), then use [negligence-analyst](../skills/negligence-analyst/SKILL.md), [professional-negligence-analyst](../skills/professional-negligence-analyst/SKILL.md), [defamation-analyst](../skills/defamation-analyst/SKILL.md), [nuisance-trespass-analyst](../skills/nuisance-trespass-analyst/SKILL.md), [misrepresentation-deceit-analyst](../skills/misrepresentation-deceit-analyst/SKILL.md) and [vicarious-liability-analyst](../skills/vicarious-liability-analyst/SKILL.md) where relevant. Reuse [product-liability-analyst](../skills/product-liability-analyst/SKILL.md), [damages-quantifier](../skills/damages-quantifier/SKILL.md) and [limitation-checker](../skills/limitation-checker/SKILL.md).

## Helpful capability categories

Use [legal research](../integrations/legal-research.md) and [document sources](../integrations/document-sources.md) within authorised scope. Preserve sensitive injury, medical and reputational evidence proportionately. Use experts for medical, technical or valuation conclusions where needed; AI legal analysis does not establish those facts.

## Execution sequence

1. Confirm represented side, conduct, loss, relationships, jurisdiction, dates, urgency and evidence. Identify immediate preservation and timing questions without inventing a deadline.
2. Apply the [civil-liability protocol](../assets/firm/civil-liability-protocol.md). Identify locally recognised causes and concurrent routes; verify elements, burdens, defences and applicable law.
3. Build a party-by-cause element/evidence matrix, showing supporting and contrary evidence, assumptions and missing proof. Do not infer negligence from injury or dishonesty from loss.
4. Test direct fault and attributed liability separately; assess alternative causes, intervening conduct, responsibility boundaries, actionable harm and defences.
5. Assess limitation, forum, remedies, mitigation, contribution and recoverable loss under verified local authority. Avoid duplicate recovery and unsupported success percentages.
6. Request proportionate specialist contributions through the Managing Partner, specifying the question, inputs and evidence needed. Reconcile conflicts transparently; repeated assertions do not verify an authority.
7. Produce a targeted evidence and expert plan, practical settlement or protective options and precise qualified local checks. Use [Dispute Viability Assessment](dispute-viability-assessment.md) when deciding whether to pursue, or [Litigation Preparation](litigation-preparation.md) for proceedings.

## Review checkpoints

1. Jurisdiction and factual coverage confirmed before selecting legal tests.
2. Elements and burdens sourced before merits conclusions.
3. Causation, alternative explanations, defences and overlapping remedies reviewed before recommendations.
4. Timing, source gaps and expert dependencies checked before final delivery.

## Verification

Verify each material local proposition, authority, pinpoint, relevant version and date under the shared research protocol. Map legal elements to actual evidence and check the strongest contrary interpretation. Do not transplant tort tests into another legal system or treat a regulatory breach, conviction, settlement or disciplinary finding as automatically determinative of civil liability.

## Escalation to human review

Escalate urgent limitation or interim-relief uncertainty, serious injury, technical standards, conflicting authority and material evidence gaps with precise questions. Separate qualified local legal review from AI counsel review. Do not republish defamatory material, contact opponents, file or commission paid experts without separate instructions.

## Deliverable

Provide an action brief, jurisdiction and date scope, party/cause/element/evidence matrix, sourced liability and defence analysis, causation and quantum assessment, remedy and timing options, evidence/expert plan, source register and explicit verification gaps. Give the represented party's strongest position and its material weaknesses.

## Fallback behaviour

If facts are incomplete, provide targeted intake and conditional issues rather than a merits opinion. Without authoritative material, identify the unverified local tests and mark law-dependent conclusions **UNVERIFIED — LIVE LEGAL RESEARCH UNAVAILABLE** where applicable. Countries without maintained modules can use bounded official-source research with disclosed limitations. Without subagents, perform the same workstreams sequentially with separately identified contributions; never claim independent review occurred.
