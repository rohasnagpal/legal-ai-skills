# Business Setup and Company Formation

## Trigger and objective

Use for “I want to start a company in [jurisdiction]”, “set up a business”, “help me incorporate” or a founder asking everything needed to launch. Produce a founder-specific structure assessment and a sourced roadmap covering formation, operational readiness and continuing duties. Registration and permission to operate are separate milestones. This is a country-agnostic workflow, not a fixed package of assumed local requirements.

## Required inputs

Use the information already supplied. Establish the target jurisdiction and material local unit, activities and regulated products, founder nationality and residence where relevant, individuals or corporate founders, ownership and control, co-founders, investment and funding, intended customers and markets, remote or local operations, staffing, premises, personal-data flows, relocation plans and target launch date. Ask for budget, commercial priorities and intended scale where needed to compare structures. Do not infer citizenship from names or conflate residence, nationality and tax residence.

For a new venture, ask for proposed particulars and available plans or term sheets. Do not require an existing registration number, constitution, statutory registers, accounts or incorporation certificate. Distinguish unavailable pre-formation records from documents that should exist for an already registered entity. A short prospective setup request is an instruction to plan: provide a useful scoped overview and material intake questions without waiting for nonexistent company records. Preserve the first-contact gate for raw narratives where it applies.

## Branches

- **First-time formation**, **foreign founder or corporate parent**, **branch or other establishment**, or **already formed but preparing to trade**.
- **Sole owner**, **multiple founders** or **investment-backed venture**.
- **Remote operation**, **local presence** or **founder relocation**.
- **Regulated or potentially regulated** versus **general business**, with the classification verified for the actual activities.

The Matter Owner records the chosen branches. Compare legal forms before assuming a company is the appropriate vehicle; include alternatives only where available and relevant under verified local law.

## Helpful capability categories

Verify current public procedures and source access. Research authority does not authorise registration, reserving names, uploading identity documents, paying fees or opening accounts.

## Execution sequence

1. Establish founder facts, activities, target jurisdiction, business model and launch objective. Supply an initial scoped overview with material questions; make conditional alternatives explicit where facts are missing.
2. Compare suitable legal forms on liability, ownership, governance, capital, local presence, tax treatment, reporting, investment and exit needs. Explain recommendations against the client's priorities and sourced rules; do not assume a private company is always best or promise tax advantages.
3. Check founder and entity eligibility, foreign ownership restrictions, regulated activities, local director or officer requirements, registered address, capital, beneficial ownership and any required professional formation intermediary. Verify each for the selected form, jurisdiction and facts rather than applying a universal checklist as law.
4. Map formation steps: name checks or reservation, founder approvals, constitutional and ownership documents, capital/subscription arrangements, officer consents, identity and beneficial-ownership evidence, registry submission and registration evidence. Distinguish legal requirements from provider practices. For multiple founders, address control, contributions, vesting where relevant, IP ownership, deadlock and exit arrangements before drafting requested documents.
5. Check operational readiness separately: sector licences, tax and indirect-tax registration, banking and payment-provider KYC, immigration and permission to work, employment and payroll, premises and local permits, privacy and cybersecurity, IP, consumer rules and essential contracts. Incorporation does not establish entitlement to a bank account, work authorisation, tax residence or permission to conduct regulated activities.
6. Use [Business Legal Inventory](business-legal-inventory.md) for systematic discovery of applicable instruments. Use [Cross-Border Business Expansion](cross-border-business-expansion.md) where founders, funding or operations create material cross-border obligations; identify home-country outbound issues without silently expanding the user's agreed country scope.
7. Build an ordered setup checklist, document pack and cost/timing schedule. Separate verified government fees, minimum capital or deposits, recurring charges and professional-service estimates. Cite official fees and their access date; label estimates and dependencies. Never promise registry processing, bank approval or licensing timelines from indicative guidance.
8. Produce a continuing-obligations calendar linked to actual triggers: statutory registers, beneficial ownership, accounts and audit where applicable, annual and event-driven filings, tax, employment, licence renewals and data obligations. Use relative timing when incorporation, financial year or another trigger is not yet known; never invent calendar deadlines. Do not create reminders or ongoing monitoring without a separate request.
9. Review structure, dependencies, source gaps and founder decisions with relevant specialists and available jurisdiction counsel through the Managing Partner. Deliver separate formation-ready and operation-ready milestones, each with outstanding checks. Label a readiness assessment conditional; do not claim registration, filing, licence issuance or professional local sign-off without evidence.

## Review checkpoints

1. Founder and activity facts established before recommending a structure.
2. Eligibility, restrictions and material tax or immigration dependencies checked before committing to a formation path.
3. Formation requirements and operational permissions reviewed as separate workstreams.
4. Current forms, fees, dates, dependencies, continuing duties and unresolved professional checks reconciled before final delivery.

## Verification

Follow the shared research protocol. Verify each material local requirement against official legislation, registry, regulator or government material actually consulted, recording provisions or relevant sections, applicable version and access date. Distinguish binding requirements, official guidance, provider policies and commercial recommendations. Verify current forms, fees, access requirements and professional restrictions before claiming submission readiness. Source status and applicability must remain separate; an unresolved requirement is not clearance. Do not make a universal completeness claim from a generic checklist.

## Escalation to human review

Identify specific qualified local verification needed for uncertain eligibility, local-presence arrangements, ownership restrictions, regulated activities, tax treatment, work authorisation, fiduciary duties and filing formalities. Explain material risk in nominee or service-provider arrangements without assuming they are lawful or suitable. AI counsel review is not independent local professional verification. Obtain separate instructions for external submissions, commitments, fees, account opening or sharing sensitive founder records.

## Deliverable

Provide a practical founder briefing with:

- A business and founder fact summary, assumptions, material questions and scope.
- A sourced structure comparison and recommendation where supported, including decisions still needed.
- A formation checklist: sequence, requirement and legal basis, documents, responsible person (proposed if unassigned), dependency, fee, expected timing and verification status.
- A separate operational-readiness checklist covering licences, tax, banking/KYC, relocation and employment, premises, data, IP and trading contracts as applicable.
- An itemised cost/timing schedule distinguishing official charges, capital commitments, estimates and recurring costs; no fabricated quotes or deadlines.
- A continuing-obligations calendar with triggers and sources, plus a prioritised local-verification brief and source/coverage gaps.

Scale detail to the business and the request. Do not interpret “everything” as a claim to exhaustive advice or bury immediate founder decisions in irrelevant legal material.

## Fallback behaviour

With incomplete facts, give a scoped overview, conditional paths and targeted questions; do not invent the founder's residence, business activity or intended entity type. Without live research or adequate authoritative records, organise the setup framework and label legal conclusions **UNVERIFIED — LIVE LEGAL RESEARCH UNAVAILABLE**. Where sources fail for one issue, retain supported work and identify that precise gap. Without registry access, do not imply a name is available, documents have been accepted or incorporation has occurred. Without subagents, perform the same workstreams sequentially.
