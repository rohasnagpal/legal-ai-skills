# Scenario Test: Civil Liability and Torts Assessment

## Prompt

> We represent a service business sued after a customer injury. The claimant alleges negligent work, a misleading assurance and responsibility for a contractor. We have the contract, incident records and conflicting witness statements. Assess the claim and defences under the identified local law.

## Expected route

Use [Civil Liability and Torts Assessment](../../workflows/civil-liability-and-torts-assessment.md), owned by Litigation with jurisdiction counsel and relevant specialists through the Managing Partner.

## Behaviour assertions

- Confirms jurisdiction, represented side, dates and actual evidence; does not infer breach from injury or employment from a contractor label.
- Separates direct fault, statements, attributed liability and concurrent contractual/statutory routes.
- Maps each sourced element to supporting and contrary evidence and missing proof.
- Tests causation, alternative explanations, defences, recoverable loss and remedies without double recovery.
- Requests technical experts where necessary and preserves unresolved disagreements.

## Verification assertions

- Pinpoints local elements, burdens, defences and limitation rules actually checked.
- Does not invent authorities, deadlines, damages or merits percentages.
- Keeps source unavailability and evidentiary weakness distinct.

## Variants

- A civil-law jurisdiction: uses the verified local civil-liability framework rather than importing common-law tort elements.
- No primary sources available: provides an unverified issue framework and verification plan, not a definitive merits opinion.
- Disputed online statement: obtains exact words and publication context, tests local defences, and does not equate criticism with defamation.
- Medical outcome alone: distinguishes adverse outcome from breach and requests appropriate professional evidence.
- Land interference: verifies standing and available lawful remedies without advising unsupported self-help.
