# Scenario Test: Arbitration Lifecycle

## Prompt

> We have a cross-border contract with an arbitration clause. Plan notice, tribunal constitution, interim relief, pleadings, hearing, award challenge and enforcement. The seat wording may be ambiguous.

## Expected route

Use [Arbitration Lifecycle](../../workflows/arbitration-lifecycle.md), led by Dispute Resolution with relevant specialists and jurisdiction counsel.

## Behaviour assertions

- Separates seat, venue, governing law, rules and institution.
- Tests conditions precedent, limitation and scope before notice.
- Coordinates appointment, interim relief, pleadings, evidence and hearing strategy.
- Maintains deadline, costs and evidence registers.
- Distinguishes award analysis, challenge and enforcement.
- Does not infer the seat or claim institutional action occurred.

## Verification assertions

- Verifies current institutional rules, lex arbitri and mandatory seat law.
- Checks challenge and enforcement deadlines from confirmed dates.
- Labels unresolved seat and asset-jurisdiction issues.
