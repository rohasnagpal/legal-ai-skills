# Scenario Test: Argentina Local Coverage

## Prompt

> We plan a business in Argentina and have supplied proposed founder, activity, location and customer details. We need formation planning, a sourced legal inventory, an assessment of customer injury exposure and a comparison with an existing overseas operation. No company has been registered yet.

## Expected route

Use [Business Setup](../../workflows/business-setup-and-company-formation.md), [Business Legal Inventory](../../workflows/business-legal-inventory.md), [Cross-Border Expansion](../../workflows/cross-border-business-expansion.md) and [Civil Liability](../../workflows/civil-liability-and-torts-assessment.md) only to the extent requested and material, with one Matter Owner and Argentina Counsel. The Managing Partner alone dispatches supporting lawyers.

## Behaviour assertions

- Establish represented side and actual facts; preserve proposed versus existing records.
- Verify Córdoba versus CABA registry and procedural competence; Spanish text and the relevant provincial sources.
- Use mapped local skills for law and neutral skills for deliverables.
- Preserve separate country findings and link each material conclusion to checked primary authority.
- Map formation and operating permissions separately; do not promise registration, licences or launch clearance.
- Test local civil-liability elements, contrary evidence and causation; do not import a foreign common-law test.

## Access and temporal variants

- A source returns an index, blank body or JavaScript shell: mark the proposition unchecked, use authorised retrieval or supplied primary text, and do not treat a snippet as authority.
- An English translation differs from the source-language version: identify the discrepancy, verify authority and version, and seek precise local review rather than silently choosing English.
- Events predate an amendment: distinguish event-date substance, procedural date and transition provisions.
- A filing trigger is disputed: record the premise uncertainty and sourced alternatives without inventing a deadline.

## Pass condition

The review trace supports the stated document/source coverage, applicability, limitations and practical conclusions. A structurally valid module or successful routing alone is insufficient to claim local-law performance.
