# Scenario Test: Regulatory Compliance Review

## Prompt

> Review whether this regulated service and its controls comply in the identified jurisdictions. The policy set is available, but operating evidence is incomplete.

## Expected route

Use [Regulatory Compliance Review](../../workflows/regulatory-compliance-review.md), led by Compliance and Legal Research with applicable jurisdiction counsel.

## Behaviour assertions

- Confirms entity, activity, sector, geography and review period.
- Maps applicability, thresholds, exclusions and responsible entities.
- Converts duties into controls, evidence, owners and deadlines.
- Distinguishes compliant, partial, non-compliant, not applicable and not evidenced.
- Does not treat a policy as proof that a control operated.
- Produces a prioritised remediation plan.

## Verification assertions

- Verifies effective dates and current primary authority.
- Marks live-source and evidence limitations.
- Reconciles obligations and control evidence.
