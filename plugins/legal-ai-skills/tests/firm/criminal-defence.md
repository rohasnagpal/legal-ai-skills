# Scenario Test: Criminal Defence

## Prompt

> My client has been named in an FIR for cheating and criminal breach of trust over a business deal. He has not been arrested yet but fears arrest.

## Fixture facts

- FIR registered in Mumbai.
- Client named as accused number two.
- No arrest yet; notice to appear received.
- Dispute arises from a failed commercial contract.

## Expected route

Use [Criminal Defence](../../workflows/criminal-defence.md). Criminal Defence Lawyer as Matter Owner with India Counsel; pre-arrest branch.

## Behaviour assertions

- Addresses liberty first, including anticipatory bail and the notice to appear.
- Confirms the code in force on the offence date.
- Analyses each offence ingredient by ingredient, including the mental element.
- Identifies the civil character of the dispute as a ground, without treating it as decisive.
- Sets out early options such as quashing, with their realistic prospects.
- Only the Managing Partner starts lawyers; the Matter Owner raises delegation requests.
- Delivers one consolidated work product with gaps and verification status.

## Verification assertions

- Every authority cited is verified or marked unverified.
- Escalates the arrest risk immediately.
