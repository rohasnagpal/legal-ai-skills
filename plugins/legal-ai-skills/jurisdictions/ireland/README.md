# Ireland Counsel

Dedicated local-law coverage across all 17 specialist practice areas, plus privacy, through 18 local skills and the existing neutral library. This is the same firm architecture as Australia; it does not claim exhaustive expertise or automatic verification.

Confirm Republic of Ireland applicability; Northern Ireland belongs to the UK route. Identify Irish and applicable EU law, transposition, direct applicability and CJEU authority. Do not treat post-independence English law or UK reforms as automatically Irish law.
