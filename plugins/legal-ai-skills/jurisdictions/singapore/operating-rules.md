# Singapore jurisdiction operating rules

These rules apply to Singapore Counsel and every mapped Singapore skill, across all practice areas.

## Jurisdiction confirmation

Confirm applicability issue by issue. Separate governing law, forum, seat, enforcement, mandatory rules and foreign-law questions. Singapore parties or an SIAC clause alone do not determine every issue. For unclear or foreign issues stop the affected local analysis and return to the Managing Partner for neutral intake or other counsel.

## Local regime and forum

Identify the competent court, tribunal, regulator or institution from current jurisdictional law and the relief sought. Check subject matter, value, persons, territorial nexus, procedural stage and date-specific transitions. Civil, criminal, family, Syariah, insolvency, specialist tribunal and arbitration regimes are not interchangeable. Do not import India's statutory workflows or UK/US procedure. Verify the civil/Muslim family-law and succession boundary per issue, not by assumption.

## Primary sources and legal status

Use [authoritative sources](authoritative-sources.md) and verify actual text. Distinguish legislation, subsidiary instruments, court rules and binding directions from guidance, consultations, institutional rules and summaries. Check the authoritative publication and commencement, not just a website's last-update date. SSO's consolidated text does not eliminate the need to check Gazette and historical provisions where material.

## Temporal law and precedent

Establish the legally relevant date and current as-of date. Verify amendments, commencement, repeals, saving and transitional provisions, procedural regime and historical versions. Identify court hierarchy, ratio/obiter, appeals, later treatment and material adverse decisions. Do not assume a complete judgment database or citator. English, Indian and other foreign decisions require a reasoned persuasive-status assessment, not treatment as Singapore law by default.

## Procedure and drafting

Verify current originating process, court forms, practice directions, service, translation/authentication, evidence, fees, filing channels, representation and applicable local professional requirements. Use local terminology and citation with provision/paragraph pinpoints; never invent a cause number, form, sworn fact, official search or completed filing. A draft remains subject to unchecked filing requirements.

## Deadlines and document coverage

Use [matter continuity](../../assets/firm/matter-continuity.md): record trigger and source, legal rule and source, inputs/result, owner, verification date and status, uncertain premises and superseded entries. Use calculators for arithmetic; calculation success cannot verify law. Record actual file/version/section coverage and source failures before affected conclusions. Follow the first-contact intake gate and the user's authorised folder and confidentiality scope.

## Escalation and limits

Identify specific missing facts, blocked primary text, uncertainty in forum or temporal law, conflicting authority, urgent time bars, unsupported technical or sector questions, and regulated steps requiring qualified local action. Do not infer universal coverage from a catalogue, but do not exclude a practice area by default. No filing, sending, payment, credential use, confidential external upload or monitoring is authorised merely by analysis.
