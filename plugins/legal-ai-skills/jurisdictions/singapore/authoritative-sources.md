# Singapore authoritative sources

Maintained starting points for all practice areas; checked on 12 November 2026. A reachable home page establishes a starting point, not verification of a legal proposition. Retrieve the actual provision, instrument or judgment at execution time. This module does not assert that every issue, private registry or historical document is publicly accessible.

## Source hierarchy and verification record

1. Applicable Constitution, legislation and subsidiary instruments in force on the material date, with authoritative publication/commencement and transitional checks.
2. Official judgments and applicable court rules/practice directions, with court hierarchy, paragraph pinpoints, appeal and later-treatment checks.
3. Regulator instruments and decisions, with their actual legal status and addressee identified.
4. Official explanatory material, followed by authorised secondary discovery sources when necessary; neither substitutes for the underlying primary text.

Record proposition, authority, provision/paragraph, legal version/effective date, official URL, retrieval date, query/coverage, outcome and unavailable checks. Do not reconstruct an inaccessible provision from snippets or an AI summary.

## Core legal text and judgments

- [Singapore Statutes Online (SSO)](https://sso.agc.gov.sg/) — statutes and subsidiary legislation, using available current/historical views. The [AGC service description](https://www.agc.gov.sg/our-roles/drafter-of-laws/singapore-statutes-online/) and [SSO FAQ](https://sso.agc.gov.sg/FAQ) explain the service and consolidation limits. Check Gazette publication and historical text where required; respect terms and reproduction conditions.
- [Government Gazette](https://www.egazette.gov.sg/) — locate official publication, commencement and notices through authorised access. Do not assume complete free archival access or that an unsuccessful search proves no notice.
- [Singapore Judiciary](https://www.judiciary.gov.sg/) — court structure, civil/criminal/family guidance and current rules/practice directions through the relevant court. Verify governing text behind a self-help guide before relying on a period or prerequisite.
- [SG Courts Judgments](https://www.elitigation.sg/gd/) — primary judgment discovery and text within its published coverage. Preserve neutral citation, court, date and paragraph. A public judgment search is not access to private case files, eLitigation filing, every oral decision or a commercial citator.
- [Syariah Court](https://syariahcourt.gov.sg/) — jurisdiction, legislation links, practice directions, circulars and process for matters within its remit. Use the applicable legislation and verify interaction with Family Justice Courts rather than generalising by religion.

## Practice-area sources

Use the corresponding local skill and neutral specialist work; the following are starting points, not claims of automatic registry/tool access.

| Area | Official starting point | What to verify |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Corporate and business | [ACRA](https://www.acra.gov.sg/) | Actual entity/filings, governance, ownership registers, approvals and reporting; correct legal form and UEN |
| Contracts and civil litigation | [SSO](https://sso.agc.gov.sg/) and [Judiciary](https://www.judiciary.gov.sg/) | Substantive authority, procedure, court competence, remedies and limitation |
| Arbitration and mediation | [SSO](https://sso.agc.gov.sg/), [Judiciary](https://www.judiciary.gov.sg/) | Statutory framework, seat, court support, challenges, enforcement; separately retrieve the agreed institution’s rules/version |
| Criminal and investigations | [Judiciary](https://www.judiciary.gov.sg/), [SPF](https://www.police.gov.sg/), [CPIB](https://www.cpib.gov.sg/) | Offence/procedure legislation, custody, charges, evidence and agency remit; announcements are allegations unless adjudicated |
| Family and succession | [Judiciary](https://www.judiciary.gov.sg/), [Syariah Court](https://syariahcourt.gov.sg/) | Applicable civil/Muslim-law framework, family procedure, protection, children, probate and succession |
| Property, tenancy and construction | [SLA](https://www.sla.gov.sg/), [HDB](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/), [URA](https://www.ura.gov.sg/) | Title/tenure, eligibility, restrictions, planning/use and applicable development or strata rules |
| Tax and duties | [IRAS](https://www.iras.gov.sg/) | Charging provisions, residence/source, GST, withholding, stamp duties, treaties and objection/appeal requirements |
| Insolvency and restructuring | [Ministry of Law](https://www.mlaw.gov.sg/), [Insolvency Office](https://io.mlaw.gov.sg/), [Judiciary](https://www.judiciary.gov.sg/) | Corporate/personal route, security/priority, moratoria, recognition and procedural requirements |
| Banking, finance and regulated services | [MAS](https://www.mas.gov.sg/regulation) | Activity scope, licences, current instruments, AML/CFT, sanctions and directories; distinguish notices, directions and guidance |
| Employment and work passes | [MOM](https://www.mom.gov.sg/), [Judiciary](https://www.judiciary.gov.sg/), [CPF Board](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/) | Worker/coverage category, entitlements, claims, work-pass rules and contributions |
| Intellectual property | [IPOS](https://www.ipos.gov.sg/), [SSO](https://sso.agc.gov.sg/), [Judiciary](https://www.judiciary.gov.sg/) | Subsistence/ownership, actual register status, territorial protection, opposition and remedies |
| Privacy and data | [PDPC](https://www.pdpc.gov.sg/), [SSO](https://sso.agc.gov.sg/) | Applicable PDPA scope, obligations/exceptions, subsidiary text, decisions, transfers and breach triggers |
| Competition, consumer and general compliance | [CCS](https://www.ccs.gov.sg/), [SSO](https://sso.agc.gov.sg/), [Judiciary](https://www.judiciary.gov.sg/) | Regime scope, unfair practices, restrictions, claim forum and remedies; CASE assistance is not a regulator judgment |
| Public and constitutional law | [SSO](https://sso.agc.gov.sg/), [Judiciary](https://www.judiciary.gov.sg/), responsible agency | Constitution, enabling power, statutory appeals, review grounds, standing and procedure |
| Legal research | All relevant primary sources above | Exact support, authority hierarchy, temporal validity and adverse/later treatment |

Institutional arbitration rules must come from the actual agreed institution, for example [SIAC](https://siac.org.sg/) if selected. SIAC rules are institutional rules, not legislation, and naming SIAC does not by itself resolve the seat. No SIAC API or search connector is bundled.

## Access observations and fallback

During this check the SSO root returned HTTP 403, PDPC presented a JavaScript verification page, and a SIAC request was blocked. Other official landing pages and the public judgment search were reachable; individual documents still need checking. The Insolvency Office currently announces a move to the Ministry of Law website on 30 November 2026; follow the official destination and preserve the accessed URL/date.

When a primary source is blocked, request the relevant official text or use an authorised browser/manual official route. Label legal conclusions dependent on unavailable material unverified and explain what needs checking. Do not bypass authentication, paywalls or anti-automation controls, bulk crawl, claim comprehensive later-treatment coverage, or upload confidential material merely to obtain a source.
