# Kazakhstan authoritative sources

Identify national, regional/local and any applicable AIFC rules separately. Determine the actual entity, activity, court and regulator nexus. AIFC connection does not establish that all national law is displaced. Check Kazakh/Russian text and its legal status; do not equate Russian-language material with Russian Federation law.

## Primary-source starting points

- [Adilet legal information system](https://adilet.zan.kz/eng): discover legislation, amendments and normative court resolutions. Use Kazakh/Russian versions and publication metadata for authority verification.
- [Law on Legal Acts: discovery starting point](https://adilet.zan.kz/eng/docs/Z1600000480). Retrieve the relevant current or historical original-language text and verify hierarchy rather than relying on the English snippet.
- [Civil Code English discovery page](https://old.adilet.zan.kz/eng/docs/K940001000_): indexed as an **unofficial translation**; verify source-language and temporal accuracy before relying on it.
- [Supreme Court government directory](https://www.gov.kz/org/sud?lang=ru): locate current official court sources and normative resolutions. Verify the actual court, binding status and procedural regime; this directory is not docket access.
- [Official government portal](https://www.gov.kz/): identify current national/local ministries and the responsible tax, employment, IP, privacy, financial or other regulator. Locate their operative instruments, not merely service summaries.
- [AIFC legal framework](https://aifc.kz/legal-framework/): distinct corporate, contract, employment, data, financial, insolvency and dispute-resolution source routes. Verify each instrument's scope, version and commencement.
- [AIFC ecosystem directory](https://aifc.kz/aifc-ecosystem/) and [public register](https://publicreg.myafsa.com/): identify current AFSA, court and arbitration routes. Public registration is not financial authorisation, national tax clearance or proof of every permitted activity.

## Access observations — 24 November 2026

Adilet's current English landing/document fetches returned a JavaScript-required shell, not the legal text. The older Civil Code English URL returned 502 on direct fetch; its indexed description identified an unofficial translation. Those observations do not verify any Civil Code proposition or the currency of the translation. The AIFC legal-framework page and ecosystem directory were readable; individual instruments, register searches and court decisions were not fully tested. The Supreme Court directory was discovered in search, not substantively inspected. Other official government and regulator routes require matter-specific access checks.

## Verification and limits

Follow the [research protocol](../../integrations/legal-research.md). Use authorised browser interaction, source-language documents or supplied official records when ordinary fetching fails; a browser-capable tool does not guarantee access. Never use a search snippet as primary authority. If original law cannot be verified, label the exact conclusion unverified and request appropriate local verification. Record issuing body, pinpoint, version, effective date, retrieval date, translation status and national/AIFC scope. Check normative court resolutions, later treatment and transition rules where material. Do not treat English availability, a source directory or AIFC registration as completed legal verification or blanket exemption.
