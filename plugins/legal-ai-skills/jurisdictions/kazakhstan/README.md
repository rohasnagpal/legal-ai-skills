# Kazakhstan Counsel

Dedicated local-law coverage across all 17 specialist practice areas, plus privacy, through 18 local skills and the existing neutral library. This is the same firm architecture as Australia; it does not claim exhaustive expertise or automatic verification.

Confirm Kazakhstan applicability, region or city, competent forum and material dates. Distinguish national law from any applicable Astana International Financial Centre (AIFC) regime, court, arbitration and regulator; do not assume an AIFC connection displaces all national law. Check Kazakh and Russian primary texts and their legal status; English translations may be unofficial or outdated. Do not import Russian law because a source is in Russian.
