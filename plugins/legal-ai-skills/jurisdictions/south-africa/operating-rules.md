# South Africa operating rules

Identify national, provincial and municipal rules, competent court/division and material dates. Consider constitutional rights, customary law and personal-law applicability on evidence; do not assume either from name or ethnicity.

Confirm governing law, procedure, arbitration seat and enforcement separately. Apply the [lawyer operating model](../../assets/firm/lawyer-operating-model.md). The Managing Partner dispatches lawyers; specialists retain matter ownership.

Verify the law at the event date and the current procedural date: commencement, amendments, repeals, savings and transitions. Identify hierarchy, binding/persuasive status and material adverse authority. Use the [source guide](authoritative-sources.md); a source listing is not a completed verification.

Verify competent forum, prerequisites, representation, rules, forms, fees, service, evidence, interim relief, review and appeal for the actual relief. Never copy an India-only workflow's statutory branch into this jurisdiction.

Follow [matter continuity](../../assets/firm/matter-continuity.md) for document coverage, changed conclusions, saved matters and auditable deadlines. Record the rule, pinpoint, trigger evidence, calculation, holidays/time zone, owner and premise uncertainty. Calculators establish arithmetic, not law.

Preserve the first-contact intake gate. Distinguish user facts, evidence, assumptions and analysis. Return source-linked practical findings and unresolved questions; identify specific steps needing qualified local professional action. No authority to file, send or upload confidential records follows from analysis.
