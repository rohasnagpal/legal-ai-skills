# Australian jurisdiction operating rules

These rules apply to Australia Counsel and every mapped Australian skill across all 17 specialist practice areas. Follow the [lawyer operating model](../../assets/firm/lawyer-operating-model.md).

## Jurisdiction confirmation

Confirm Australian applicability issue by issue. Identify the relevant state or territory, Commonwealth law, governing law, procedural forum, arbitration seat, enforcement location and material dates separately. Australian residence or incorporation alone does not resolve all applicable law. Return unclear or foreign issues to the Managing Partner for neutral intake or other counsel.

## Commonwealth, state and territory law

Check the applicable level of government for each obligation and remedy, including concurrent laws, application laws, referrals, exclusions and transitions where relevant. Cover NSW, Victoria, Queensland, Western Australia, South Australia, Tasmania, ACT and Northern Territory as facts require. For external territories verify the particular legal arrangements. Never present a state rule as an Australia-wide rule. Address local government, native title, Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander interests and specialist regimes where the facts raise them.

## Authority and temporal law

Use the [source guide](authoritative-sources.md). Verify legislation, instruments, rules, commencement, amendments, historical versions, savings and transitions against the legally relevant date. Record current as-of dates separately. Identify court hierarchy and whether each proposition is binding or persuasive, ratio or obiter. Check later treatment and adverse authority within available sources. Foreign decisions require an explicit relevance assessment. Disclose incomplete databases and unavailable citator access.

## Forum and procedure

Identify the competent federal, state or territory court, tribunal, regulator or institution for the actual persons, relief and procedural stage. Verify jurisdictional limits, review routes, originating process, practice directions, service, evidence, forms, fees, filing channels and representation. Distinguish merits review, judicial review and statutory appeal. Check Western Australian family forum arrangements and national versus state employment coverage where relevant. Use the actual local procedure when adapting the firm's workflows.

## Sources and drafting

Distinguish legislation, judgments, court rules and binding instruments from regulator guidance, consultations and summaries. Record authority, provision or paragraph, version or effective date, official URL and retrieval date for every material proposition. Use Australian citation and local terminology subject to the intended forum's requirements. Do not invent forms, case numbers, facts, source access or completed searches.

## Deadlines and document coverage

Follow [matter continuity](../../assets/firm/matter-continuity.md). Record rule and source, trigger and evidence, calculation, holidays and time zone where relevant, owner, verification date, premise uncertainty and superseded entries. Calculators perform arithmetic after legal premises have been verified. Preserve actual file, version and section coverage, access failures and the first-contact intake gate.

## Escalation and access

Identify specific uncertainty in jurisdiction, law, adverse treatment, evidence, source availability and urgent deadlines. Request other expertise through the Managing Partner. Identify steps requiring qualified local professional action. Follow the user's authorised document scope and privilege constraints. Analysis alone does not authorise filing, sending, payment or confidential external upload. Use the [integration guide](integrations.md) for access limits.
