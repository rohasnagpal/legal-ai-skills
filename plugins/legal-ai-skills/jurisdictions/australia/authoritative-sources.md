# Australian authoritative sources

## Legislation

Use the authorised Commonwealth register and the official service for each relevant state or territory. Confirm the instrument's status, version, commencement, amendments, historical text and transitions at execution time. The directories below identify official starting points. Listing a website does not verify a legal proposition or guarantee access.

- [Federal Register of Legislation](https://www.legislation.gov.au/).
- [Office of Parliamentary Counsel state and territory legislation directory](https://www.opc.gov.au/links/legislation-states-territories-and-new-zealand).
- [New South Wales legislation](https://legislation.nsw.gov.au/).
- [Victorian legislation](https://www.legislation.vic.gov.au/).
- [Queensland legislation](https://www.legislation.qld.gov.au/).
- [Western Australian legislation](https://www.legislation.wa.gov.au/).
- [South Australian legislation](https://www.legislation.sa.gov.au/).
- [Tasmanian legislation](https://www.legislation.tas.gov.au/).
- [ACT legislation](https://www.legislation.act.gov.au/).
- [Northern Territory legislation](https://legislation.nt.gov.au/).
- [Territory arrangements](https://www.legislation.gov.au/help-and-resources/understanding-legislation/territories).

## Courts, tribunals and procedure

- [High Court judgments](https://www.hcourt.gov.au/cases-and-judgments/judgments).
- [Federal Court](https://www.fedcourt.gov.au/).
- [Federal Circuit and Family Court](https://www.fcfcoa.gov.au/).
- [Family Court of Western Australia](https://www.familycourt.wa.gov.au/).
- [Administrative Review Tribunal](https://www.art.gov.au/).

For state and territory matters retrieve judgments, rules, practice directions, forms and fees from the actual court or tribunal's official site. Establish the hierarchy and jurisdiction before using precedent. AustLII and other discovery services may help locate material. Verify important propositions against official text where available and report any unavailable text or later-treatment check. Public judgments do not establish private file or filing access.

## Practice-area starting points

| Work | Official starting points and required local checks |
| --- | --- |
| Corporate | [ASIC](https://www.asic.gov.au/), Commonwealth legislation, constitution and authorised company records; check foreign investment and local obligations where relevant. |
| Contracts, civil claims and arbitration | Applicable legislation and judgments, the actual court's procedure and the agreed institution's published rules; verify the seat and domestic or international framework. |
| Criminal and investigations | Relevant Commonwealth or state or territory legislation, actual court, investigating agency and prosecutor; verify offence, powers, bail and evidence regimes. |
| Family | Relevant legislation, FCFCOA, Family Court of WA and applicable local courts; check protection, child protection, adoption and succession separately. |
| Property | Relevant legislation, land registry, planning authority and state or territory revenue office; use authorised title records and check foreign investment where material. |
| Tax | [ATO](https://www.ato.gov.au/), Commonwealth legislation, official treaty text and the relevant state or territory revenue authority; distinguish rulings and guidance from enacted law. |
| Insolvency | ASIC, [AFSA](https://www.afsa.gov.au/), legislation and competent court; distinguish corporate insolvency and personal bankruptcy. |
| Banking and finance | ASIC, [APRA](https://www.apra.gov.au/), [AUSTRAC](https://www.austrac.gov.au/), legislation and [PPSR](https://www.ppsr.gov.au/) where relevant to the activity. |
| Employment | [Fair Work Commission coverage guide](https://www.fwc.gov.au/apply-or-lodge/who-australias-national-workplace-relations-system-covers), [Fair Work Ombudsman](https://www.fairwork.gov.au/), legislation and applicable state authorities, awards and agreements. |
| IP | [IP Australia](https://www.ipaustralia.gov.au/), legislation, judgments and authorised records; verify the type of right and territorial scope. |
| Compliance | Responsible sector regulator, legislation, instruments and licence conditions; distinguish binding obligations from consultations and guidance. |
| Privacy | [OAIC](https://www.oaic.gov.au/), Commonwealth and relevant state or territory legislation and local privacy or health regulators; verify coverage, exemptions and commenced reforms. |
| Consumer | [ACCC](https://www.accc.gov.au/), legislation, local consumer agency, tribunal or court; verify the applicable remedy and forum. |
| Public law | ART, competent court, governing enactment and decision maker; verify merits review, judicial review or statutory appeal separately. |
| Research | Official legislation, judgments and relevant regulator materials, with a proposition-level verification ledger and adverse-authority checks. |

## Verification record

Record the proposition, authority, provision or paragraph, jurisdiction, version, material date, official URL, retrieval date, confirmed or assumed status and any unavailable check. A source catalogue is a research route, and each matter requires actual text verification. Report conflicting authorities, blocked text and incomplete later-treatment coverage before the affected conclusion.
