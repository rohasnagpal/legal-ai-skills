# Australia Counsel

Australia Counsel is included in the single Legal AI Skills plugin alongside India, US, UK and Singapore Counsel. It supplies Australian law and procedure across **all 17 specialist practice areas**, using the same Managing Partner, Matter Owner, verification and human-review model. Eighteen local skills supplement the existing neutral skills and workflows; they do not replace the specialists or claim exhaustive expertise in every niche law.

## How to use

Ask an Australian legal question, name the represented party and provide relevant documents and dates. The Managing Partner uses [Australia Counsel](../../skills/australia-counsel/SKILL.md) to identify the local regimes, forum and specialist owner, then verifies local law during work and at final review. Quick matters still use the smallest suitable skill. Cross-border matters receive a separate local-law layer from each relevant counsel.

Examples: Australian arrest/bail; Commonwealth and state or territory family issues; a property purchase; an employment claim; tax objections; a creditor restructuring; IP licensing; a consumer dispute; judicial review; a regulatory investigation; an acquisition, contract, arbitration or privacy programme. All are within the jurisdiction route; actual advice depends on verified law, evidence and source access.
