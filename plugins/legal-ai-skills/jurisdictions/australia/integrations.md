# Australian integrations and registries

Australia uses the host's available authorised official-website research tools and supplied records. This module adds jurisdiction instructions and skills. The source guide identifies websites to consult in each matter.

- Verify the actual access and search coverage of every research tool. Public website availability does not establish a dedicated Australian MCP connector, registry entitlement or commercial citator access.
- For ASIC, ABN, land titles, PPSR, IP and licence records, establish the correct identifier, record type, date, authorised access and any fee before describing a completed search. Distinguish ACN and ABN and reconcile entity names. Search absence does not establish clearance.
- Separate public judgments from private court files and electronic filing. Verify the competent forum and any access restrictions.
- Use existing calculators after verifying Australian legal premises, holidays, cutoffs and time zones. Document tools depend on the engines actually installed.
- Existing sanctions connectors cover their stated lists. Check the relevant Australian sanctions sources separately when required, including the [DFAT sanctions material](https://www.dfat.gov.au/international-relations/security/sanctions).
- Preserve query, source, identifier, version, retrieval time and result status. Respect privilege, credentials, terms and authorised document scope. Obtain authorisation for paid records or confidential external uploads.

When access fails, record the specific outstanding proposition and work from authorised supplied text within its temporal scope. Follow the [legal-research protocol](../../integrations/legal-research.md) and [matter continuity](../../assets/firm/matter-continuity.md).
