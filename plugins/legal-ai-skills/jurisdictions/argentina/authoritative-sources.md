# Argentina authoritative sources

Confirm the province/CABA, municipality, federal or local competence, relevant dates and legal subject. Spanish primary text controls the research; label translations and verify the underlying wording rather than importing Spanish law.

## Primary-source starting points

- [National legislation: InfoLEG](https://www.infoleg.gob.ar/) and [official legislation search](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa).
- [Official Gazette](https://www.boletinoficial.gob.ar/): publication, amendments and operative instruments; follow provincial gazettes for local rules.
- [Civil and Commercial Code: updated text starting point](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/ley-26994-235975/actualizacion). Check amendment and commencement history for the relevant date; this listing does not verify any particular article.
- [Supreme Court decisions](https://www.csjn.gov.ar/decisiones) and [official court/provincial judgment routes](https://tramites.csjn.gov.ar/herramientas/sentencias). Determine court and judgment authority rather than treating summaries as holdings.
- [IGJ](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/justicia/igj) and [local registry directory](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/justicia/registro-nacional-sociedades/registros-locales). Verify actual registry jurisdiction; do not apply CABA registration requirements nationwide.
- [ARCA](https://www.arca.gob.ar/) for national tax/customs discovery; locate provincial/CABA and municipal revenue sources separately.
- [AAIP](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/aaip) for personal-data and information-access instruments.
- [INPI](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/inpi) for industrial-property research. Verify copyright sources separately.
- [BCRA](https://www.bcra.gob.ar/) for financial/currency instruments; identify the competent securities, labour, consumer, competition, environment or other regulator from the actual activity and official government directory.

## Access observations — 24 November 2026

InfoLEG, the national legislation-search landing page, Official Gazette, Supreme Court decisions page, IGJ and AAIP pages were retrieved as readable pages. ARCA returned no readable text through the research fetch. Other links are discovery starting points, not claims of completed searches or provision verification. Individual court databases, registry transactions, regulator instruments, INPI/BCRA pages and provincial portals were not comprehensively tested. Recheck source availability per matter.

## Verification and limits

Follow the [research protocol](../../integrations/legal-research.md). Retrieve actual instruments, provisions, judgments and operative versions; preserve pinpoint, URL, issuing body, date, translation status and access status. Verify federal/provincial competence, local procedure, amendments, commencement and adverse authority. Regulatory announcements and proposed reforms are not operative rules. Use the relevant national or local primary source for each specialist issue; gaps remain explicit where courts or regulators cannot be accessed. A country module is not exhaustive local expertise, a commercial database entitlement or professional sign-off.
