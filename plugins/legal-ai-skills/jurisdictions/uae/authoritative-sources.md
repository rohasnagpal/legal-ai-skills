# UAE authoritative sources

Identify the emirate, mainland or particular free zone, federal/local forum, DIFC or ADGM nexus and governing language. Do not treat all free zones as DIFC or ADGM. Determine the authoritative Arabic text and status of any English translation.

## Primary-source starting points

- [Federal legislation](https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en)
- [Federal/local judicial framework](https://u.ae/about-the-uae/the-uae-government/the-federal-judiciary)
- [Ministry of Justice](https://www.moj.gov.ae/)
- [Dubai legislation](https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/)
- [DIFC laws](https://www.difc.com/)
- [DIFC Courts](https://www.difccourts.ae/)
- [ADGM legal framework](https://www.adgm.com/legal-framework)
- [Federal Tax Authority](https://tax.gov.ae/)
- [Central Bank](https://www.centralbank.ae/)

## Research and access rules

Official starting points were identified on 24 November 2026. The primary legislation portal returned HTTP 403 to automated retrieval; its text was not verified in this session. Recheck access for each matter; these observations are not a promise of ongoing availability.

Retrieve actual legislation, gazettes, amendments, commencement instruments, judgments, rules and practice directions. Verify consolidated-through dates; an official portal can lag recent changes. For regional law use the relevant legislature, gazette and court; national text alone is insufficient. For specialist duties follow the responsible regulator's official instrument, distinguishing guidance from binding rules. Public judgments do not provide private docket or filing access.

Use the [central research protocol](../../integrations/legal-research.md). Record proposition, authority, pinpoint, material date/version, URL, retrieval date and status. Check adverse authority and later treatment where accessible. Secondary databases are discovery aids; disclose gaps in official text and citator coverage. Never invent a provision or infer verification from a search snippet.
