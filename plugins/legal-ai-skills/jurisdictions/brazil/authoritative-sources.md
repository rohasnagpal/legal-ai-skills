# Brazil authoritative sources

Confirm Brazil applicability, state, municipality, competent forum and material dates. Distinguish federal, state and municipal competence and specialist justice branches. Verify authoritative Portuguese text, amendments and translation status; do not import Portuguese or other Lusophone law.

## Primary-source starting points

- [Federal legislation](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/)

## Research and access rules

The federal legislation index was accessible on 24 November 2026. This checks the directory only, not underlying legal propositions. Recheck access for each matter; a directory entry does not verify law. Follow the [research protocol](../../integrations/legal-research.md). Retrieve the actual primary text, official gazette and responsible court or regulator instruments; for local rules use the relevant local authority. Record proposition, pinpoint, version, effective date, URL, retrieval date, translation status and access gaps. Do not infer verification from snippets or AI summaries.
