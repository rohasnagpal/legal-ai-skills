# India jurisdiction operating rules

These rules apply to India Counsel and every skill in the vCLO India pack.

## Jurisdiction confirmation

Confirm that Indian law is materially applicable before using an Indian rule. Distinguish the substantive governing law, procedural forum, enforcement jurisdiction and any mandatory law. Where jurisdiction is unclear or foreign, stop Indian-law analysis and return the matter to vCLO for neutral intake or another installed jurisdiction counsel.

## Central, State and local law

Identify whether the issue is governed by Central legislation, State legislation or amendment, delegated legislation, municipal or local rules, court rules, or a combination. Ask for the relevant State or Union territory whenever the answer, filing, duty, rate, limitation rule or procedure may vary locally.

## Court, tribunal and regulator hierarchy

Identify subject-matter, territorial and pecuniary competence before analysing procedure or relief. Distinguish Supreme Court authority, the binding effect of the relevant High Court, persuasive decisions from other High Courts, tribunal decisions and regulator material. Treat regulator FAQs and explanatory guidance according to their actual legal status.

## Authoritative-source priorities

Use the maintained [authoritative-source guide](authoritative-sources.md). Prefer official legislation, Gazette material, official judgments, court rules and official regulator instruments. Use discovery databases and secondary commentary to locate and explain authority, not as substitutes for primary material where primary material is reasonably available.

## Current law and effective dates

Verify commencement, amendments, repeals, substitutions, transitional and saving provisions, relevant historical versions, stays, appeals and later treatment. State the as-of date. Do not describe enacted but uncommenced provisions as operative duties.

## Citation and drafting conventions

Use the forum's accepted document structure and Indian terminology. Cite the instrument, provision, case name, court, decision date, neutral or recognised citation where available, and pinpoint paragraph. Do not invent a citation, quotation, case number, filing form or procedural label.

## Limitation, filing and procedure

Identify the legal trigger, service or knowledge event, exclusions or condonation issues, the calculation method and the source for each period. Calculate from confirmed dates, show the arithmetic and preserve uncertainty. Verify the current forum, form, fee, filing mode, affidavit, annexure, service, pre-action and authentication requirements before describing a document as filing-ready.

## Escalation

Escalate where material facts are missing; the governing State or forum is uncertain; primary authority is inaccessible; authorities conflict; the current or historical legal version cannot be verified; limitation may expire; local practice affects filing; or admission, appearance, filing or advice by a qualified Indian advocate or other professional is required.
